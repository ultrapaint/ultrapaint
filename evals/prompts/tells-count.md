Read every picture and the source. For each mark, list every place the page has it, as the words
it holds or the element it is (`"Built for teams"` over the pricing heading; the three feature
cards), and nothing that only resembles it:

- `eyebrow`: a small label set over a heading (often uppercase, tracked or in the accent), news,
  proof and a story's category included: each one;
- `numbers`: numbers over or beside section headings ("01", "02 / Features") whose sections are
  not steps, a timeline or a ranking;
- `gradientText`: words filled with a gradient;
- `iconTiles`: a row of cards each opening on an icon in a tinted or outlined rounded square
  above its heading: one per row;
- `sideStripe`: a thick accent border down one side of a card, callout or row;
- `glow`: a coloured or zero-offset blurred shadow under a card or a button;
- `borderAndShadow`: a card with both a `1px` border and a wide soft shadow;
- `fragments`: two fragments set against each other in the copy ("Not a tool. A platform.");
- `buzzwords`: an adjective any competitor could claim, in a headline or subhead (seamless,
  powerful, next-generation, effortless, supercharge).
