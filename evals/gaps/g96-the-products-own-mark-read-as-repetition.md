**What happened.** Asked to redesign the slash between the editor's mark and the project's button,
the agent recommended removing the mark: it opens nothing, and the browser tab, the window and the
splash already name the product, so by the restraint rules it repeated them. The person: "That's
completely wrong. I want to keep our logo, maybe slightly smaller so it aligns with the rail items
size below? But still I want it, and I probably want a divider between it and the project picker".

**The skill now.** `SKILL.md`, Restraint: "Every element earns its place. A label, description,
caption, heading, tooltip, badge or icon that says what the content already shows is noise", and
Icons: "A brand mark keeps its official form." Nothing says a product's mark in its own chrome has
a job of its own; the agent read "says what is already shown" across surfaces (the browser's tab,
the window) rather than within the screen.

**Proposed.** In `SKILL.md`, Restraint: "The product's own mark at the head of its chrome is
identity, not repetition: keep it, sized and placed with the chrome around it; what repeats is
judged within the screen, not against the browser or the window." A redesign asked of one element
changes that element; removing its neighbours is offered only as an alternative, not recommended.

**Test.** A review on an app shell whose sidebar starts with the product's mark beside a workspace
switcher, asked to improve the header: the mark is kept in every run.
