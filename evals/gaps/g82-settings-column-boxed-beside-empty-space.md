**What happened.** On a 1440px window, every settings screen of `rows-nov19` (12 of 12, both
versions) set its content left-aligned beside the navigation, 740 to 960px wide, leaving 184 to
420px empty on its right, while the top bar ran full width to the window's edges. The person, on
the notification screens: "I don't like the layout meaning that the whole content is boxed while
we have more space in the page. We could make it wider or have the whole content centered (also
since the navbar is full width to the edge and it's not boxed). This could be something we should
work on since it's not the first time this happens." Pictures in `gaps/g82/`.

**The skill now.** It asks for this layout:

- `references/kinds/settings.md`: "On a desktop the content column sits left-aligned beside the
  navigation, `720–1090px` wide, never a narrow column centred on the page"; Check: "A narrow
  settings column centred on the page → Left-aligned beside the navigation, `720–1090px` wide".
- `SKILL.md`, Layout: "a product's main column takes a container token (about `768px` for prose
  and forms …)".

**Proposed.** Not yet worked out. Two ways the person named: the column wider, or the content
centred in the space beside the navigation. A form's rows read badly when the label and its
control drift far apart, so the likely line: the column keeps its width and is centred in the
space beside the navigation. Outside guidance to be read first.

**Test.** The notification and preference briefs, 2 runs, Opus designing, against the shipped
skill: the empty band on each side at 1440 and 1920 measured from the render, and the person's
eye on the pairs.
