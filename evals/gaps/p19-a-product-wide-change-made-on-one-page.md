**What happened.** On a client app a redesigned shared element (a switcher in the header) was drawn
in one of its two variants only; a rule the person set for every page's width was applied to the one
page in view each time; colour-role changes reached the page under review and not the rest of the
canvas. The person asked "Did you updated all screens in this canvas?", and the agent said it had
left the others "because the review only covered this page". The person repeated such a request 5
times in 4 conversations.

**The skill now.** `SKILL.md`, Usability: "One action has one name, one variant and one place across
screens; it keeps its place and form once used, empty or not." The rule holds across screens, but no
step of the design job says to carry a change to every screen that has the same thing. The editor's
briefing gets that line in UP-247.

**Proposed.** Measured once UP-247's briefing line ships: if the fault still recurs, a line in the
design job's step 5 (Check): "A change that sets a rule for the product (a width, a colour's job, a
shared element) is made on every screen and state that has the same thing; the last message names any
left and why."

**Test.** Multi-page briefs on a canvas of 6–10 screens, then a follow-up that changes a shared
element or a page rule, 2 runs each at Opus: the share of screens that carry the change, with the
briefing line alone against the briefing line and the candidate.
