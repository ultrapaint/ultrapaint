# Restraint

Every element earns its place. A label, description, helper text, caption, heading, tooltip, badge
or icon that says what the content already shows is noise, and noise makes a design worse, not
safer. Agents add it by habit, so it is the first thing to look for.

## Structure explains

Alignment, grouping, repetition and position say what belongs together and what matters before
any word does. When a design needs words to explain its structure, the structure is what is
wrong: fix the grouping, not the caption.

## What noise looks like

- A card titled "Total revenue" over "$41,295 total revenue"; a figure with a caption that names
  it again.
- A description under a heading that restates it ("Notifications: manage your notifications").
- A line explaining a control whose label or icon already says it: "Click to upload" under an
  upload area, "Toggle to enable" beside a switch.
- An intro paragraph, eyebrow or subtitle nobody asked for, on a screen whose purpose is plain.
- A heading over a group its content makes obvious (an "Actions" heading over two buttons, a
  "Workspace permissions" heading over three switches that each name a permission); a label
  repeated on every row that a column header says once.
- Decoration: an icon beside every label, a box around a single element, a divider between things
  already apart, a badge that says nothing.
- An icon inside a field whose label already names it (an envelope in an email field), an icon
  before every item of a tree or a list of names.

The fix is to remove it, not to shrink it or grey it out.

## Content leads, chrome recedes

The data, the text and the primary action are the heaviest things on the screen; frames,
borders, backgrounds and navigation are the quietest. Where the chrome draws the eye first, take
weight out of the chrome, not the content.

## Alignment is exact

Nested items line up on one column: an item under a group starts where the group's items start.
Icons share one style, one corner radius and one way of drawing a dot or a badge.

## Before you finish

| You see | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A heading, caption or description that repeats what is beside it | Remove it |
| A sentence explaining how to use a control | Remove it; the control's label says it |
| An intro, eyebrow or subtitle on a screen whose purpose is plain | Remove it |
| A heading over a group its content already names | Remove it; a surface or space holds the group |
| An icon on every label, a box around one element, a divider between separated groups | Remove the decoration |
| Navigation or borders heavier than the content | Lighter chrome |
| An item under a group starting at another column | One column for the group's items |
