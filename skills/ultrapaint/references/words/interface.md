# Interface copy

The words of a product's own screens, case by case: labels, buttons, confirmations, errors,
empty states, links, settings, dates and numbers.

## Labels and buttons

Both short: "Invoices", "Send invoice", "Save draft". Uppercase is a text style laid over
sentence-case text, never a way of writing it.

## Confirmations

"Delete 3 invoices?" with `Delete invoices` and `Cancel`: the buttons alone answer the question,
and the title names what is lost.

## Errors and hints

One or two sentences, next to what they are about: "Couldn't save the invoice. Check your
connection and try again." A hint says what to do ("Use only letters"), not what is forbidden
("No numbers or symbols"). The user is never to blame.

## Empty states

What this place holds, how to fill it, one action: "No projects yet", "Projects keep your tasks
and files together.", `Create project`. A filtered one names the query and a way out: "No results
for ‘quarterly’. Clear filters".

## Links

"Read the billing docs", "Learn more about exports": each makes sense read alone. When a whole
card is the link, its title is the link text, and no "Read more" sits under it.

## Settings

A toggle: "Send read receipts", not "Read receipts enabled".

## Dates and numbers

"2 Oct 2026" or "2 minutes ago"; relative times for what just happened, absolute ones for what is
planned. Thousands separated, the currency with the amount: "€1,240.00". A placeholder shows an
example of the format: `name@example.com`.

## Words the screen already says

A caption that repeats what sits beside it goes: "vs last year" after a change, "Enabled" beside
a switch, "17 rows" under a table, "5 of 5" over a stepper, a title over a toolbar. A plain value
the brief writes as text ("Moderate") stays text, not a coloured badge.

## Check

| You see | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Are you sure?" | The question names what is lost: "Delete 3 invoices?" |
| "Something went wrong" | What happened and what to do |
| A hint that lists what is forbidden | What to do |
| A filtered empty state with no way out | The query named, and "Clear filters" |
| "Read more" under a card that is already a link | The card's title as the link |
| A date as `2026-10-02` | "2 Oct 2026" |
| A caption restating the thing beside it | Remove it |
| A plain value turned into a coloured status badge | Plain text |
