# Voice

How the product sounds and what it calls things: one voice on every screen, its tone moving with
the moment.

## The product's choices come first

Read the copy the product already has before writing: its screens and the strings in its code.
DESIGN.md sets the voice: the tone in its Overview (terse, warm or the brand's), and in its Content
section how its words sound, the one name of each thing, the words it never uses for it, and its
formats. Plain language bends to that voice everywhere except errors and destructive moments.
Where DESIGN.md chose nothing, `references/words/interface.md` and `references/words/pages.md`
hold.

## A tone for the moment

| Moment | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Success, onboarding, empty states | Warm, may be light |
| Routine actions, settings | Neutral, minimal |
| Errors, destructive confirmations | Calm and plain |
| Data loss, security, money | Serious and explicit |

## One vocabulary

A thing keeps its name across the product: a workspace in the menu is a workspace in the toast.
A flow keeps one set of moves: "Continue" or "Next" throughout, and "Done" to finish.

## Speak to the reader

"You", not "the user". No gender by default, no idiom that needs a culture to decode, "select"
where both touch and pointer apply.

## Check

| You see | Fix |
