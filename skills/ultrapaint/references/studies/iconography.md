# Icons: what well-made sites do

The counts cover 4,983 sites: 996 technical, 982 humanistic and 3,005 expressive. The particulars come from notes on 900 of them. Every percentage below is from the counts. Every "in the notes" figure is a tally of the sample.

## What holds across characters

### One style per site, and that style is outline

| style | all (4,983) | technical (996) | humanistic (982) | expressive (3,005) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| outline | 2,323 · 46.6% | 625 · 62.8% | 553 · 56.3% | 1,145 · 38.1% |
| none | 1,326 · 26.6% | 62 · 6.2% | 101 · 10.3% | 1,163 · 38.7% |
| filled | 1,046 · 21.0% | 254 · 25.5% | 234 · 23.8% | 558 · 18.6% |
| illustrated | 270 · 5.4% | 45 · 4.5% | 89 · 9.1% | 136 · 4.5% |
| duotone | 16 · 0.3% | 10 · 1.0% | 5 · 0.5% | 1 · 0.0% |

- **Outline is the default.** It is the most common style overall and in technical and humanistic sites. Outline sites outnumber filled sites by about 2.2 to 1.
- **Do not reach for duotone.** It appears on 16 sites of 4,983.

### Fewer icons than you think

- **A quarter of sites carry no icon style at all** (1,326, or 26.6%).
- **Many more carry only one glyph.** The most frequent written uses are:
  - "none": 819 sites, plus 36 "none visible"
  - "hamburger menu only": 23
  - "logo mark only": 5
  - "thin arrows after text links": 5
  - "chevrons on nav dropdowns": 5
  - "only a close × on the announcement bar": 3
- **Start from no icons and add one only where it says something faster than a word.**

### Where icons earn their place

Across the notes, the same few places recur:

- **Header utilities:** search, account, cart or bag, wishlist, language globe.
- **Menu markers:** a chevron on a dropdown, a hamburger.
- **Direction on links:** a small arrow after a text link, or inside the primary call to action.
- **Carousels:** prev and next arrows, often in thin circles.
- **Media controls:** play, pause, mute, a sound toggle.
- **Dismissal:** a close × on a modal or banner.
- **Lists that compare:** a check for included, a dash or cross for absent, a small "i" for a tooltip.
- **Status:** a dot or tick beside a word.
- **Format badges on content cards:** headphones for an audio story, play for video, a gallery mark. The title cannot say these, so the badge does.

### Some glyphs are filled by nature

These are filled on outline sites without breaking the style:

- star ratings
- a play triangle
- a status dot
- a check circle in a pricing row
- third-party brand marks (Apple, GitHub, Google, X, Instagram)

These glyphs do not count as a second style. A second drawing style for ordinary glyphs does.

### Brand marks keep their own form

Platform and integration logos sit as they are: in white circles, on chips, inside "Download for Mac" and "Sign up with Google" buttons. Do not redraw them in the system's set, and do not tint them to the accent.

### Small, in the text's colour

- In navigation, utilities and links, icons are small and take the colour of their label.
- Colour appears only where the icon is the graphic of a tile (see the recipes).

### Moving content carries a pause

Logo marquees, autoplay carousels and hero videos in the notes repeatedly carry a small pause control. Add one wherever something moves on its own.

### Draw icons as SVG, not an icon font

One site in the notes shows a broken glyph above "App Of The Day", where an icon font failed to load.

## By character

### Technical: outline, small, mostly inside the product

- **62.8% outline**, the highest share of any character.
- **Only 6.2% carry no icons.** Only 18 sites (1.8%) record no icon use at all. A technical page almost always has some.
- **Most of its icons are the product's own.** The notes keep naming icons that live inside the product screenshots: "sidebar navigation in the app mock-up", "toolbar controls inside the screenshot", "inside the product mockup only". These belong to the product, not to the page. Draw them in the product's set at the product's sizes.
- **On the page itself, technical sites keep to:**
  - nav dropdown chevrons
  - an arrow after "Learn more" or inside the button
  - a search field with ⌘K
  - check marks in feature and pricing lists
  - a GitHub mark with a star count in the nav
- **Brand marks are frequent and filled.** Integration logos orbiting a central mark and logo chips in prompt boxes are how technical sites show "works with". They account for much of the 25.5% filled.

### Humanistic: outline, plus the largest share of illustrated sets

- **56.3% outline, 23.8% filled.**
- **9.1% illustrated**, twice the share of the other two characters. Examples in the notes:
  - hand-drawn spot icons beside problem items
  - engraved antique objects as nav icons
  - inked badges above value props
  - single-line drawings marking process steps
- **An illustrated set replaces the line set for a region; it is not mixed into it.** The illustrated values row or step row sits beside an ordinary outline header (search, cart) only because those are utilities, not content.
- **Shops** (the largest humanistic genre in the notes, about 60 sites):
  - Nearly 30 of them carry the same header row: outline search, account and cart, sometimes wishlist, locale or chat. Icon-only, right-aligned.
  - Below the header: filled star ratings under products, hearts on product cards, circular carousel arrows.
- **Restaurants and bars** (about 20 in the notes):
  - More than half carry no icon or a single one: a location pin before the address, a close × on the booking modal, one download arrow beside "menu PDF".
  - Navigation is words.
- **Games and game microsites** (about 17 in the notes):
  - Usually a title screen with one control.
  - About 5 carry a sound toggle, as a small outlined square in a corner or as text ("SOUND: \*ON\*").
  - Otherwise: store badges, a play button, and the game's own pixel or HUD glyphs.

### Expressive: no icons is the most common answer

- **38.7% carry no icon style**, the largest single value in any character.
- **780 sites (26%) record no icon use at all.** Another 20 use "hamburger menu only".
- **Typography does the icons' work:**
  - a typographic arrow inside the text ("↳ Browse all work", "Still Water ↗")
  - bracketed labels ("[ SEE COLLECTION ]", "[ BUY ]")
  - a word in place of a control ("Menu", "play", "unmute", "Sound off")
  - an index number in a corner
- **Portfolios and studio sites** (the largest expressive genre in the notes) mostly carry nothing beyond:
  - a two-line hamburger
  - a thin arrow beside a project link
  - an Instagram glyph
- **Editorial magazines** (about 12 in the notes) are the exception that needs icons:
  - search and account in the masthead
  - a small format badge on story cards: headphones on Wired, play and audio on Vox, podcast and gallery markers on the Harvard Gazette
  - a share rail
- **Fashion and homeware shops** keep the shop header row (search, wishlist, bag) even inside an expressive design.

## Recipes

1. **A technical feature tile** (about 10 technical sites in the notes)
   - One outline line glyph, white on a solid accent rounded square or dark on a pale tinted square, at the top-left of the card.
   - Under it, a bold title and a two-line grey caption.
   - Laid out in a 3-up or 3×3 grid, every card with a different glyph.
   - Where the tile can show a cropped piece of the real UI instead, the stronger sites do that and drop the glyph.
2. **A pricing or comparison table** (nearly every one of the ~14 pricing pages in the notes)
   - A thin outline check for every included feature.
   - A faint grey dash or cross for absent.
   - A small round "i" before feature names that carry a tooltip.
   - In the recommended column, the checks invert to white on its fill.
   - No other icons on the page.
3. **A shop header** (nearly 30 humanistic shops in the notes)
   - A centred wordmark, with tiny uppercase tracked text links on the left.
   - Icon-only outline search, account and bag on the right, with the cart count as a small circle or "[0]".
   - Below the header:
     - circular outline arrows for product carousels
     - filled stars with a review count under product names
     - an outline heart at the top-right of product tiles
4. **An expressive page without icons** (1,163 sites with no icon style)
   - Navigation in words: corner labels, a comma-separated line of links, or a "Menu" pill.
   - Calls to action as underlined text with a typographic arrow.
   - One close × on the cookie or newsletter modal.
   - At most a two-line hamburger.
5. **An experience gate** (games, brand experiences and documentaries in the notes)
   - A full-screen scene.
   - One start control.
   - A sound toggle as a small outlined square or word in a bottom corner.
   - Optionally a thin scroll chevron.
   - Nothing else until the visitor enters.
6. **An editorial card with a format badge** (about 12 magazines in the notes)
   - A small square or round filled badge on the thumbnail (headphones, play) or a tiny outline glyph before the uppercase kicker.
   - Only on items whose format differs from a plain article.
