# Spacing on well-made sites

This reference comes from a study of 4,983 websites chosen by people for being well made. Every number below is from the counts across all sites. Every particular is from a sample of 900 sites with notes. The sites are websites, mostly landing pages, brand sites, shops and editorial pages. Where a rule is about the screens of a tool rather than its site, it says so.

## What holds across characters

### Leave room: airy or balanced, rarely dense

Of 4,981 sites, 2,819 are airy (57%), 1,888 balanced (38%) and only 274 dense (5.5%). Start every page balanced or airy. Density is a choice a page has to justify, and 225 of the 274 dense sites are expressive.

### Sections sit about 120 px apart

The median gap between sections is **120 px**, measured on 4,093 sites. Most sampled sites fall between 80 and 200.

- Use 120 as the page's largest regular step.
- Go below it for editorial fronts and shop grids.
- Go above it only for pacing (see Expressive below).

### Surfaces are padded about 28 px

The median padding inside a card, panel or band is **28 px**, measured on 2,506 sites. That is roughly a quarter of the section gap.

The ratio matters more than the values. Space between sections is about four times the space inside a surface. This is the skill's rule that groups sit at least twice as far apart as their items, applied at page scale.

### Many pages have no padded surface at all

Only 2,506 of 4,983 sites show a measurable padding. The rest set their content in full-bleed bands, gutterless grids and bare text on the page.

A surface is earned by a job to hold (principle 3). It is not the default container for every section.

### A gap, a rule or a colour change: one at a time

890 of 4,983 sites record no section gap. These are single-screen pages, or bands butted edge to edge. In the notes, these sites separate sections in one of two ways instead of with space:

- **A change of band colour:** Mount Sapo, Fly By Jing, Piecework Puzzles, The Cozy Hollow.
- **A shared 1 px rule:** Dabbit's edge-to-edge cell grid, Inkredible Notary's "shared 1px borders instead of gaps", and the developer-auth feature box whose "cells share 1px dividers instead of using gaps".

Each of these replaces the gap. None adds to it. Never draw a rule across a gap that already separates two things.

### Text keeps a narrow measure inside wide sections

Content columns run about **1,170–1,240 px**. Text inside them stays much narrower. Sampled sites use:

- centred paragraphs of about 470 px;
- a manifesto column of 680 px;
- an essay column of about 600 px;
- feature columns of about 550–660 px.

Let the section span the width. Keep the text to roughly 480–700 px.

### Inset panels keep an even margin from the viewport

A rounded hero or panel inset from the window keeps the same small margin on every side. Sampled sites use 8 px (Orbi), 12–14 px (Hedera Dx, a healthcare platform), 15–16 px (Norma, OffCourt), 20 px (Lowe's Innovation Labs, Vestris) and 24 px (Aave).

Pick one value and use it for every panel on the page.

## Per character

### Technical: balanced, with the widest section gaps

Rests on 996 sites:

- **Density:** 549 balanced (55%), 430 airy (43%), only 17 dense (1.7%).
- **Section gap:** median **130 px**, the widest of the three characters (n = 966).
- **Padding:** median **28 px**. It is recorded on 833 sites (84%), more than in any other character. Technical sites put content on surfaces: bento cards, bordered feature grids, pricing cards.

A technical product's *site* is precise and calm, not compact. Particulars from the notes:

- **Bordered grids with cells joined by hairlines**, not separated by gaps. Examples are a 790 px panel split into two columns by 1 px dividers, and logo walls of equal ruled cells.
- **Dashed or hairline guides framing the content column.**
- **Bento cards padded 24–48 px**, with a product crop bleeding off one edge.

The compactness the character skill describes (rows of 32–40 px, tables first) belongs to the tool's own screens.

### Humanistic: the most padding inside surfaces

Rests on 982 sites:

- **Density:** 488 balanced (50%), 462 airy (47%), 32 dense (3%).
- **Section gap:** median **120 px** (n = 884).
- **Padding:** median **30 px**, the highest of the three (n = 614).

Roominess lives inside cards and panels, not between sections. A humanistic page is not more spread out than a technical one. It is softer inside each surface.

Two genres in the sample differ enough to name:

- **Shops (fashion, beauty, food, homeware), dozens in the sample:**
  - mostly balanced, with sections about **90 px** apart;
  - padding often 0;
  - borderless product tiles on a tinted fill;
  - gutters from none (Everyday Humans) to 2–4 px (RAQ) and 8 px (Pedestal).
- **Hospitality (restaurants, hotels, bars), about thirty in the sample:**
  - airy, with sections 110–160 px apart and padding mostly 0;
  - full-bleed photo bands;
  - photos matted in a 14–20 px frame (Jacob's Cove, Allta's 16 px gutter with about 110 px between photos);
  - text in narrow centred columns.
- **Games and game microsites** are usually one fixed screen with no section gap at all (Equinox, World Chess, BEIGE FORCE, KPR).

### Expressive: most airy, most dense, fewest surfaces

Rests on 3,005 sites:

- **Density:** 1,927 airy (64%), 851 balanced (28%), 225 dense (7.5%). The character polarises.
- **Section gap:** median **120 px**, but recorded on only 2,243 sites (75%). A quarter of expressive pages are single screens or butted bands.
- **Padding:** median **24 px**, the smallest, and recorded on only 1,059 sites (35%). Most expressive pages have no padded surface at all. Type, imagery and position do the grouping.

Particulars from the notes:

- Portfolios anchor small text to the four corners of an empty viewport, inset about 14 px (Niccolò Debole, Ben Wegscheider).
- Manifestos put 300–400 px or more between statements.
- Agencies stage a full-screen band holding one small paragraph as a pause (Monolog).

Two genres differ enough to name:

- **News and magazine fronts (about fifteen in the sample: Wired, Vox, Mic, Mother Jones, Harvard Gazette, Coda Story):**
  - balanced to dense, with sections **40–90 px** apart;
  - padding 0;
  - three columns split by vertical hairlines;
  - stories separated by horizontal rules, not cards.
- **Portfolios and studio sites (dozens in the sample):**
  - airy;
  - either one screen (gap 0) or work bands 140–300 px apart;
  - text at one or two sizes.

## Recipes

1. **Technical landing page.**
   - Balanced.
   - Sections 120–160 px apart in a column of about 1,200 px.
   - Features in one bordered panel whose cells share 1 px dividers instead of gaps.
   - Cards padded 24–32 px.
   - A logo wall of equal ruled cells directly under the hero.
   - *Examples:* the developer-auth platform (airy, 160 / 48), PlanetScale-style database pages (balanced, 160 / 32).

2. **Stacked inset sheets.**
   - Rounded panels inset 8–24 px from the viewport on a darker or tinted page.
   - Padding 40–60 px.
   - Panels touching or 16–24 px apart, so the page edge frames each sheet like a bezel.
   - *Examples:* the crypto-wallet page (120 / 48), the GTM data SaaS (24 / 48, stacked pastel panels), OffCourt (16 px inset and gaps, 40 padding), Norma (15 px inset, 56 padding).

3. **Tight bento.**
   - Gutters of 4–16 px.
   - Padding 24–48 px.
   - Each tile one tone off the page.
   - Text padded evenly; the product crop bleeds off the bottom edge.
   - *Examples:* Herding (16 px gutters, 45 padding), The Workshop Survival Guide (6 px gutters, 40), Spor (12 px gutters, 48), Wild Week (4 px gutters, 28).

4. **Editorial front page.**
   - Dense or balanced.
   - Sections 40–90 px apart.
   - No cards and padding 0.
   - Three columns divided by vertical hairlines, with stories stacked between horizontal rules.
   - *Examples:* Wired (dense, 60 / 0), Vox (dense, 60 / 16), Harvard Gazette (balanced, 70 / 0), Mother Jones (balanced, 60 / 28).

5. **Hospitality band page.**
   - Airy.
   - Full-bleed photo bands about 110–160 px apart.
   - Padding 0.
   - Text in a narrow centred column between photos; photos matted in a 14–20 px frame.
   - *Examples:* Allta (110, 16 px gutters), The Ellison (140), Kereliott (160), Jacob's Cove (20 px matte).

6. **Single-screen statement.**
   - Airy, with no section gap.
   - One block of text or one object centred in an empty viewport.
   - Labels pinned to the corners at an even 14–30 px inset.
   - Everything at one or two type sizes.
   - *Examples:* Niccolò Debole, Middle Child (30 px corners), Hugo & Marie. A scrolling variant pauses 300–700 px between statements (Telescope, the healthcare-AI manifesto at 400 px).

## Before you finish

| You see | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A technical product's landing page set with 32 px rows and 48 px between sections | Balanced: about 130 px between sections, about 28 inside cards |
| Every section of an expressive page boxed in a card | Remove the boxes; let type, image and position group |
| Sections 120 apart with a hairline drawn across each gap | Keep the gap or the rule, not both |
| A wide section whose paragraph runs the full 1,200 px | A 480–700 px text column inside the wide section |
| Inset panels with different margins from the viewport | One inset value, 8–24 px, for every panel |
| A card padded 16 px on a marketing page | 24–32 px |
| Card text touching the edge where a screenshot bleeds | Keep text padded; only the image passes through the edge |
