# Layout: what well-made sites do

This reference rests on 4,983 sites chosen by people: 996 technical, 982 humanistic and 3,005 expressive. Particulars come from notes on 900 of them, up to 300 per character. Every number below is from the counts unless it says "notes".

Two limits shape what it can tell you:

- **The sites are websites,** mostly marketing, shop, brand and portfolio pages captured at 1440 px. Admin tables, settings screens and phone layouts are not in the study. The skill's rules for those stand untested.
- **Expressive sites are 60.3% of the study,** so the all-site figures lean expressive. Read the per-character figures before the totals.

## 1. What holds across characters

### A page is a stack of bands on one column
- **The two commonest structures are bands and a single column.** Full-bleed bands lead with 1,180 of 4,983 sites (23.7%). A single column follows with 1,125 (22.6%). Together they are 46%.
- **Backgrounds bleed; content keeps one width.** The content column has a median width of **1270 px** (n = 4,983).
- **Separate sections by changing the band's surface,** not by adding dividers or headings. In the notes, a band turns cream, then dark, then tinted.

### Reading text is far narrower than the page
- **Prose sits in a narrower column inside the 1270 px container.** In the notes, centred paragraphs run about 470 px, essay and manifesto columns 560–680 px, and long-reads about 750 px.
- **Keep the two widths apart.** The page container holds bands and grids. The measure holds text.

### The hero carries a visual
- **About three heroes in four show something besides text:** 3,756 of 4,983 (75.4%).
  - Text and visual: 1,283 (25.7%)
  - Full-bleed image: 1,060 (21.3%)
  - Video or 3D: 577 (11.6%)
  - Illustration: 455 (9.1%)
  - Product screenshot: 375 (7.5%)
- **Text-only heroes are 1,047 (21.0%).** Sites with no hero at all are 180 (3.6%).

### Alignment has no default; each section picks one and holds it
- **The three answers split almost evenly:** left 1,803 (36.2%), centred 1,632 (32.8%), mixed 1,548 (31.1%).
- **"Mixed" in the notes almost always means a centred hero over left-aligned sections.** It does not mean several edges inside one section.

### Rules and surfaces hold the structure, not boxes and headings
- **Hairlines do most of the dividing.** Full-width hairline rules and visible column lines recur in every character in the notes. So do bands of a different tint and tiles a shade off the page.
- **Cards are rarely outlined.** A tile is told apart from the page by its tone.

### Scrollers peek, media insets
- **A carousel lets its next item run off the edge.** In the notes it is cut by the viewport or by the column, often with round arrow buttons.
- **A hero panel often sits inset,** 8–24 px from the viewport, with a large radius (about 20–56 px). That frames it without a border.

### One primary action
- **Pair one filled button with a quieter one.** In the notes the secondary is an outline, a grey fill or a text link with a chevron, and both buttons share one height and radius.

### Technical: the product is the picture
- **Use a narrow, centred, single-column page.** It has the narrowest median width (1200 px) and the most single columns.
- **Make the product the hero image.** Technical sites own the product-screenshot hero: 285 of the 375 in the whole study (76.0%). A full-bleed photo is rare here: 44 (4.4%).
- **Bento is a technical layout.** Technical sites hold 76 of the study's 148 bentos (51.4%), though they are only 20% of the sites.
- **Freeform and editorial columns are almost absent:** 12 and 11 sites.

Genres in the notes:
- **SaaS landing pages (most of the 300):**
  - A pill eyebrow carrying an announcement link above the headline (about 25 notes).
  - A two-tone headline: a dark claim, then a grey continuation at the same size (about 30).
  - A logo strip in one grey straight under the hero (about 30).
  - Feature rows that alternate sides (about 25).
  - Dashed or hairline guide lines framing the column like a blueprint (about 20).
- **Pricing pages (about 15 notes):**
  - Plans in one row with one recommended plan.
  - A monthly/annual segmented toggle.
  - A comparison table with hairline rows, a check where a plan has the feature and a dash where it does not.
- **Help centres (about 8 notes):**
  - The search field is the hero.
  - Articles are hairline rows: a title, one grey line, a chevron.
- **Blogs (about 10 notes):**
  - One wide split card for the featured post, above a three-column grid.
  - "Category · date" in grey above each title.

### Humanistic: photographs and bands
- **Build the page from bands.** Bands are 39.2% of humanistic sites, the highest share of any character.
- **Let a photograph be the hero.** Full-bleed photographs are 37.1% here, against 4.4% for technical sites.
- **Centre the hero and left-align the sections.** Alignment is the most often mixed and the least often left. Left is only 24.7%.

Genres in the notes:
- **Restaurants, bars and hotels (about 25 notes):**
  - A centred wordmark with the nav split left and right of it.
  - Labels in small, widely tracked capitals.
  - A condensed or high-contrast serif headline.
  - One deep ink colour on cream.
  - Menus as hairline rows with the price right-aligned.
- **Shops (about 70 notes):**
  - Borderless product tiles: a photo on a tint that matches the page, with the name and price beneath.
  - 50/50 bands of a photo and a flat colour panel, alternating sides.
- **Games (about 15 notes):**
  - One fixed screen that does not scroll.
  - A title lockup and one large play button.
  - A sound toggle in a corner.
  - Nothing else.

### Expressive: compositions, not templates
- **Freeform and editorial columns belong here.** Expressive sites own 505 of the study's 549 freeform pages (92.0%), 210 of its 233 editorial-column pages (90.1%) and 439 of its 577 video or 3D heroes (76.1%).
- **The work can be the whole first screen.** Expressive sites hold 157 of the 180 sites with no hero (87.2%).
- **Product screenshots and bento are rare:** 27 and 39 sites.

Genres in the notes:
- **Portfolios and studios (about 100 notes):**
  - A wordmark set edge to edge.
  - Labels of 11–14 px pinned to the corners of the screen.
  - The work index set as a hairline table.
  - Often one fixed screen; in about 30 notes both captures were identical.
- **Editorial magazines (about 15 notes):**
  - Three unequal columns.
  - Hairline rules instead of cards.
  - A tiny uppercase kicker above each serif headline.

## 3. Recipes

**A technical product hero.**
- **Structure:** a single centred column 1040–1300 px wide (single-column 333, centred 337 of 996; median 1200).
- **Eyebrow:** a pill only when it carries a real announcement and links to it.
- **Headline:** two-tone, the claim in near-black and the continuation in muted grey at the same size.
- **Actions:** a dark filled pill beside a light grey pill or a text link with a chevron, of equal height.
- **Social proof:** one row of customer logos in a single grey, with no caption above it.
- **Product:** a screenshot wider than the text column that overhangs the hero band's lower edge, so it straddles the two surfaces. This is the commonest technical hero: 285 sites.
- **Variant:** a few notes set the headline at only 26–30 px and let a full-width screenshot carry the screen.

**A technical feature section.** Use one of two forms, both resting on technical counts.
- **Split rows (172 sites):**
  - Text in one half, a cropped piece of real UI in the other.
  - The sides alternate from row to row.
  - The UI sits on a flat tinted slab offset behind it.
- **A bento (76 sites):**
  - Tiles one shade off the page, with 6–16 px gutters in the notes.
  - Each tile has a title and one grey line at the top left.
  - A UI crop bleeds off the tile's bottom edge.
  - No border, no icon row.

**A pricing page.** Grid structure, notes only (about 15).
- **Plans:** three or four in one row, each in the same order: name, price, one line of description, button, features.
- **Price:** large, with the unit (/mo) small and grey beside it.
- **Recommended plan:** marked once, by the only filled button or the only dark card. Sites often stack three marks (a tab, a border and a fill); one is enough.
- **Billing toggle:** a segmented control with the savings stated beside it.
- **Comparison table:** hairline rows, a check or a dash, the plan names in a sticky header.

**A humanistic shop or hospitality page.** Full-bleed bands (385) over a full-bleed photographic hero (364); median width 1250 px.
- **Header:** a centred wordmark with the nav split either side, in 11–13 px widely tracked capitals.
- **Headline:** a serif display headline, condensed or high-contrast, sitting low over the photograph.
- **Colour:** one deep ink colour on cream.
- **Bands:** 50/50 photo and flat-colour panels, alternating sides.
- **Product tiles:** borderless.
- **Menus and price lists:** hairline rows, name on the left, price right-aligned.

**An editorial front page.** Editorial columns (210 expressive sites).
- **Columns:** three of unequal width, with the lead story at about two-thirds width or in the centre.
- **Rail:** stacked stories separated by hairlines, not cards.
- **Each story:** a tiny uppercase kicker in sans or mono, a serif headline, a grotesque standfirst, then a small-caps byline.
- **Breaks:** a full-width light-grey band separates sections.

**A studio or portfolio.** Freeform (505) or no hero (157).
- **Wordmark:** the name is the headline, set edge to edge, at 190–430 px in the notes.
- **Labels:** everything else at one size of 11–14 px, often mono, pinned to the corners of the screen.
- **Actions:** no buttons. The nav is text links.
- **Hero:** the work itself.
- **Index:** a hairline table of year, client and project.

## Before you finish

| You see | Fix |
|---|---|
| A landing page squeezed into 768 px | A page container of about 1200–1300 px. Keep 768 px for prose and forms. |
| A paragraph stretched across 1270 px | A reading measure of about 560–760 px inside the band. |
| A technical marketing hero with no product in it | A real screenshot or crop, overhanging the band's edge. |
| An expressive page built as a bento of cards | Compose it: freeform, editorial columns or asymmetric bands. |
| An eyebrow over every section, a caption over the logos | Remove them. |
| Sections divided by a heading and a box | A band of a new tint, or a hairline rule. |
| A carousel that ends flush with the column | Let the next item run off the edge. |
| Centred and left edges mixed inside one section | One alignment per section. Only the hero may differ. |
