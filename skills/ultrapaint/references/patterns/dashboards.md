# Dashboards

This is how one reference UI kit builds dashboards, counted over the 56 dashboard pieces in a study of 575. A share is a share of those 56 pieces. "~n notes" means at least n, because each note records only a few things about a piece. Where a pattern differs from the design skills, the skills win. The person's principles win over both. A pattern here is common; that does not make it right.

**KPI cards**
- The card holds:
  - a small muted label, often itself a dropdown with a chevron (~8 notes);
  - a large figure of about 36 px;
  - a small trend badge, right-aligned on the figure's baseline (~15);
  - sometimes a sparkline, a kebab or a six-dot drag handle (~5).
- The selected KPI drives the chart. A grey fill, an underline or an offset shadow marks it (~8).
- ~6 notes add "vs last month" after the badge. The skills drop that caption, because the badge already says it.

**The page figure**
- A hero figure such as a balance or revenue is set at about 48 px.
- Emphasis comes from colour within one size: a dark value and a muted unit.

**Header**
- A breadcrumb sits above the title, and the actions are right-aligned to the title. 61% put actions in the header.
- Only 13% have a description under the title, the fewest of any kind.
- Page titles reach about 36 px.
- In ~4 notes, the title carries its context muted at the same size, for example "Analytics" followed by the site's domain.
- In ~6 notes, every header button is an outlined secondary, with no filled primary. A screen built for reading often has no filled primary at all (~10 across dashboards and tables).
- ~6 give every header button an icon. The skills keep an icon on at most one.

**Time range**
- Usually a segmented control (~14).
- Otherwise plain text toggles, "12m 30d 7d 24h", with the active value dark (~8).

**Sections**
- Grouping is 55% mixed and 21% cards.
- Each widget sits in its own bordered card in ~20 notes.
- In ~12, sections have no cards. Space and a rule under each title hold them.
- ~6 use a two-layer card: a grey title strip over an inset white panel. This draws one edge twice, and the skills leave it out.
- A section title carries an inline metric or count in ~6: "Site traffic +104%", "Customers 68".

**Charts**
- The current period is solid; the previous one is dashed or dotted (~11).
- A forecast or second series is hatched (~9).
- Only greys or one hue (~6). Series are neutral; they never take the accent.
- No legend in any of ~25 charts: dash and hatching tell the series apart.
- The tooltip is dark and rounded, with label/value rows (~8).
- No y-axis labels in ~3.

**Tables in dashboards**
- The first cell is an avatar or logo, with the name over a muted email or domain.
- Status is an outlined pill with a coloured dot (~10). Across kinds, this neutral badge holds in ~25 notes.
- Selecting rows raises a floating dark bulk bar: "3 selected · Export · Archive · Delete" (~5).
- Rows with two-line identity cells run to about 72 px (~3, from tables).

**Search empty states (~8 notes, mostly search menus)**
- The layout is centred:
  - an illustration of what the search would have found, such as an avatar fan or logos on rings;
  - a bold title;
  - a two-line muted line that quotes the query;
  - one secondary "Clear search".
- ~5 offer two buttons, and ~4 repeat the recovery as an inline link too. The skills keep one action.
- ~4 admin and finance screens use 3D folder illustrations. A technical tool stays plainer than that.

**Spacing, type and icons**
- Padding 24, between groups 32, within groups 12.
- **5 type sizes and 3 weights**, tied with marketing for the most of any kind. The skills cap them lower.
- Fields are placeholder-only in 86%.
- Leading icons in fields: 71%, mostly search magnifiers.
- One icon style: a 2 px outline at 20 px.
