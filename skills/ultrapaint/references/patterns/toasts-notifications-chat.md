# Other: toasts, notification panels, AI chat, file managers, auth and contact pages, icon sets

This is how the reference library builds these pieces. It is counted over the 99 pieces of this kind in a study of 575. A share is of this kind's pieces, and "≈n" means at least n. Where a pattern differs from the design skills, the skills win, because they record what a person preferred when the two were compared. The person's principles win over both. A pattern here is common, which does not make it right.

This kind is a mix, so only the sub-patterns that repeat are reported. Across the whole kind the medians are 4 type sizes, 2 weights, 24 padding, 16 within groups and 32 between groups. 54% of headers carry a description.

**Toasts (≈6 here, ≈10 more inside other pieces)**
- A leading icon or file-thumbnail column comes first. Then a semibold title and a muted description at the same size.
- The actions are text only. They sit under the text, on the text column: a muted "Dismiss" first, then a brighter or accent positive action ("Undo", "View in finder"). This holds in ≈14 of ≈16.
- A close X sits top-right (≈12). When one is present, "Dismiss" does the same job a second time.
- The surface is often inverted near-black and sits bottom-right (≈8).

**Notification panels (≈6, plus 2 menus and 1 dialog)**
- A 40 px avatar sits in a fixed left gutter.
- The first line is a sentence. The actor and the object are semibold; the verb is regular and muted (≈8). A muted meta line follows: "time • team" (≈7).
- Attachments, quoted comments and Accept/Decline buttons indent to the **text column, not the avatar** (≈8).
- Unread is a small green or blue dot, not a row fill (≈6).
- The tabs are a segmented control, with a count on the active tab (≈5). The footer has Close on the left and "Mark all as read" on the right.

**AI chat panels (≈7)**
- The two speakers are told apart by fill and offset, not by hue. One side is white with a border; the other is grey and indented (7/7).
- Hovering over a message shows a dark floating icon toolbar (≈6).
- The composer is a bordered box over a grey footer strip. A model or user select sits on the left; "Shortcuts" and "Attach" sit on the right as text (≈6).
- A confirmation is two equal buttons or two text buttons (≈5).

**File managers (≈4)**
- Four quick-create cards: an icon tile top-left, a + top-right, and the label bottom-left (4/4).
- A file row is an icon tile, the name, then "size | type" split by a thin rule. Pinned cards reuse the same anatomy (4/4).

**Editors and CMS pages (≈6)**
- The toolbar starts with selects (font, size), then icon groups split by short vertical rules (6/6).
- Tooltips show the shortcut ("Bold ⌘B", ≈5). Selecting text raises a floating toolbar (≈4).

**Slide-outs (≈5)**
- The header holds a featured icon tile, a title, a description and a close X. A tile on its own is decoration under the principles.
- The footer is pinned under a full-width top rule: a tertiary link on the left, Cancel and Save on the right.

**Auth and contact pages (≈20)**
- They follow the forms pattern: a split screen with a form column of about 360 px, a full-width near-black primary, and a centred line for switching flow.
- In some forms the placeholder stands in for the label (≈10). Others centre the title over left-aligned labels (≈5). The skills win on both.

**404 pages (3)**
- A two-column hero: text with a search field or links on the left, an illustration on the right. The footer sits on a grey band.

**Icon style sheets (≈27)**
- These are marketing visuals, not interface. One glyph comes in 4 styles. The stroke is 2 px with round caps on a 24 px grid. Solid icons cut their details out as negative space. A modifier sits bottom-right with a gap cut around it.
