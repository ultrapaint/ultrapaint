# Landing pages

How well-made SaaS landing pages are built, counted over 270 slices of real pages: heroes, feature sections, pricing, FAQs, testimonials, closing calls to action and footers. A "(counts)" figure is either a share of the pieces of one kind or a median. "≈n notes" means at least about n notes. Where this reference differs from the design skills, the skills win, because they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared. Where it differs from the principles, the principles win over both. A share says a pattern is common. It does not say the pattern is right.

## Marketing sections (138)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** mixed 48%, space 26%, cards 22%, dividers 4%.
- **Actions:** inline 64%, none 22%, in the section header 12%, a full-width footer 2%.
- **Headings:** 65% carry a description. Titles have a median of 4 words, descriptions 17 words.
- **Type:** a median of **5 sizes and 3 weights**. This is the only kind with three weights.
- **Primary buttons:** a median of **2**. The nav repeats the hero's action, so it is one action shown twice.
- **Spacing:** 16 px within a group, 90 px between groups and 24 px container padding. Between-groups is about 5.6× within.
- **Icons:** decorative 60%, in buttons 54%, in navigation 43%, in list rows 32%, on labels 24%, on headers 19%, leading a field 8%.
- **Fields:** placeholder only in 52%.

### Hero (≈35 notes)

**Order.**
1. An optional eyebrow or announcement pill (≈25 notes).
2. The headline.
3. A muted subtitle of 1–2 lines at 17–22 px, 10–40 px below the headline.
4. The actions, 24–40 px lower.
5. An optional reassurance line (≈8 notes).
6. The product visual or a logo strip.

**Alignment.** A centred stack is the most common hero. The rest split the copy on the left and a product visual on the right, or set a left-aligned stack over a photo.

**Headline.**
- 2–4 lines at 56–110 px, with tight negative tracking and a line height near 1.0.
- The weight splits evenly: ≈12 notes are light or regular, ≈14 heavy or black. Neither is the pattern's default. The typography skill's default of regular or medium holds.

**Two-tone headline (≈15 notes).** One part of the headline is set apart, and never more than one:
- a grey second line that does the subtitle's job (grey for the setup, the foreground for the payoff), or
- one word or line in the accent.

**CTAs.**
- A **pair** in ≈16 notes: a filled primary beside a secondary of the **same height and shape**. The secondary is an outlined pill, a pale grey or tinted fill, or a translucent fill on photos. It is a text link with an arrow in ≈4.
- The primary is near-black rather than the brand colour in ≈8.
- ≈6 heroes have one CTA and 3 have none.
- The header repeats the main action, smaller, in ≈8.

**Above the headline.** An eyebrow or announcement pill (a "NEW" chip, a link, an arrow) appears in ≈25 hero notes. An announcement bar above the nav, with an arrow link and a dismiss ×, appears in ≈9. Keep one only when it states a fact the headline does not.

**Below the CTA.** A one-line reassurance at 12–16 px, muted ("No credit card required"), in ≈8 notes. It earns its place only when it adds something.

**Single-field capture (≈7 notes).** An email or username field fused with its button in one pill or row of the same height, placeholder only. The accessibility skill still asks for a visible label, or at least an accessible name and a button that names the action.

**Product visual.** Real product UI in ≈20 notes: a framed screenshot, a device bezel, a tilted stack or floating fragments. It is often cropped or bleeds off an edge.

**Logo strip (≈20 notes).**
- Greyscale or monochrome in ≈14, brand colour in ≈5.
- A small muted "Trusted by…" caption in ≈8.
- Marquees fade at the edges. 2 notes give them a pause control.

### Feature sections (≈60 notes)

**Section header.**
- An optional eyebrow, a 36–64 px headline (a median of about 48), and a 1–2-line muted lede capped at about 580–600 px.
- In ≈8 notes it is split: the headline on the left, the description on the right.

**Alternating split rows (≈12).**
- Text and media swap sides on each row.
- Rows are **120–300 px apart, with no rules**.
- The text column is 40–50% wide and either vertically centred or top-aligned with the media.

**Bento grids (≈12).**
- Filled cards (light grey, pastel or near-black) at **12–32 px radius**, with no border or a hairline.
- Gutters are **16–32 px** and padding is 32–48 px.
- A mock UI sits on top of the card or bleeds off an edge.
- The title and 1–3 muted lines are anchored bottom-left.
- The layout skill keeps expressive pages off a bento of cards.

**Feature columns without cards (≈10, 3–4 across).**
- A small line icon (sometimes none) over a 16–20 px semibold title and 2–3 muted lines.
- The iconography skill starts an expressive page with no icons.

**Body and lead-ins.**
- Feature body copy is set large, 17–22 px on a 28–40 px line height (≈10).
- A bold lead-in phrase runs into grey body copy in place of a separate title (≈8).

**Section changes.** A change of band colour, with no rule, in ≈25 notes.

**Links.** Text links end in an arrow or chevron and mostly name where they go ("View all integrations →"). "Learn more" alone appears in ≈6.

### Pricing (≈12 notes)

**Card stack, in one order (5 notes).**
1. The plan name.
2. A large price at regular weight, with a small muted unit on its baseline.
3. A one-line billing note.
4. A full-width button.
5. An "All X features, plus:" lead-in.
6. A checklist.

**Alignment.** Buttons sit on one row across cards whatever the list length, and the cards share one height (3).

**Recommended plan.** Marked once in 3 notes (a small tag, or a tab fused to the top edge). Marked two or three ways in 2. Once is the skill's rule.

**Billing.** A segmented toggle with the discount inside the active segment (4). A usage slider with labelled ticks (3).

**Tooltips.** Feature terms carry a dotted underline or an "i" icon (5).

### FAQ (≈13 across kinds)

**Layout.** A narrow centred column 630–1030 px wide, or two columns with the title or categories on the left and the accordion on the right (4).

**Rows.**
- 57–105 px tall, with questions at 16–28 px.
- The toggle sits at the far right: a plus (7), a chevron (4) or an arrow (1).
- Full-width hairlines separate the rows in 8 notes. In 5, each row is its own filled or bordered card, 12–16 px apart.
- No answer shows while a row is collapsed.

### Testimonials (≈12 notes)

- **The quote is the headline**, at 20–48 px and often in a display or serif face.
- The attribution is 13–16 px and muted: the name over the role.
- Layouts are carousels with the next card cut off at the viewport edge (6), often by a third to a half, or masonry walls (3). The layout skill's 16–32 px peek holds.
- Key phrases are highlighted in 2.

## Navigation: footers and closing CTAs (39)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** space 44%, mixed 33%, dividers 15%, cards 8%.
- **Container padding:** 48 px, the most of any kind. 17 px within a group, 64 px between groups.
- **Headings:** 21% carry a description. Titles have a median of 1 word.
- **Type:** 4 sizes, 2 weights.
- **Icons:** decorative 62%, in buttons 46%, on headers and labels 5%.

**Footer layout (≈28 notes).**
- The logo sits alone in a left column, with a tagline or trust badges.
- **3–6 link columns** fill the right on an even grid (≈12).

**Column heads.**
- Small, muted, uppercase or mono at 11–13 px in ≈12. In ≈5 of those they are lighter than the links.
- Dark and semibold at 13–18 px in ≈10.
- None in 2, where meaning groups the columns.

**Links.**
- 13–15 px at one size and weight, with no icons.
- A 22–49 px pitch, with a median of about 35.
- Long labels wrap rather than widening the column (3).

**Legal row.** The copyright, policy links separated by pipes or dots, social icons and a language select. A full-width hairline sits above it in ≈8 notes, and space alone separates it in ≈5.

**Extras.**
- A giant or cropped wordmark as the sign-off (≈6).
- A tiny tinted "NEW" pill after a link (4).
- A language select drawn as a pill with a globe or flag (5). A quieter text-and-chevron version fits the principles better.
- The surface is a dark band, a rounded inset panel, or the page's own warm off-white (≈6).

**Closing CTA (≈12).**
- A 44–72 px headline, an optional muted line, and **one small button** or a matched pair.
- Either centred, or left-aligned with the button on the right of the same row (3).
- 2 pages end on the statement with no button.

## Table: grids and comparison tables (42)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** cards 29%, dividers 29%, space 24%, mixed 19%.
- **Actions:** none 50%, inline 40%, header 10%.
- **Headings:** 54% carry a description. Titles have a median of 3 words.
- **Spacing:** 16 px within a group, 60 px between groups, 25 px padding.
- **Type:** 4 sizes and 2 weights.
- **Fields:** placeholder only in 87%.

**Comparison tables (≈10).**
- **Hairline row dividers, with no cell borders and no zebra stripes.** The lack of stripes is stated in 3.
- Rows are a uniform 40–71 px, and they grow when a value wraps rather than truncating it.
- Labels are left-aligned and small (13–17 px). Plan columns are equal and centred.
- **Included** is a check. **Not included** is quiet in 5 notes (an empty cell, a faint dash, a grey minus) and a cross in 3, all of them brand-against-competitor tables.
- The brand's or recommended column is **one continuous surface** (a band, or an outline through every group) in 5, not a badge.
- Category rows are a filled band, a short empty row or a muted caption on a rule (5).
- A footnote closes the table (3).
- An "i" on every row (4) is a candidate for removal.

**Post grids (≈10).**
- **No card surface in 7**: a rounded image, with the text straight on the page.
- The stack is a category chip, a 2–3-line title at 17–24 px in medium or semibold, an optional 2-line muted excerpt, and a meta row with an avatar, a name and a date.
- Colour carries the hierarchy more than size does (2).
- In list form, rows put the thumbnail on the left. Hairlines sit about 30 px clear of the text, and the whole row is the link with no "Read more" (2).

**Feature grids (≈10).**
- A flat light-grey fill with no border, a 12–24 px radius and 40–48 px padding.
- A 17–28 px title over 2–3 muted lines.
- One card in a row is inverted to near-black for rhythm (2).

**Customer stories (5).**
- A logo stands in for the heading.
- The sentence itself is the link (2), or a link is pinned to one baseline (2).
- A "Case study" tag under each logo repeats what the grid already says.

## Other (33)

**(counts)**
- **Actions in the header:** 18%, the highest of any kind.
- **Fields:** placeholder only 67%, labelled 17%, with helper text 6%.
- **Spacing:** 16 px within a group, 64 px between groups, 24 px padding.
- **Type:** 5 sizes.

**(notes)**

**Help centres (4).**
- Search is the hero in 3: a pill about 640 × 64 px with a magnifier, placeholder only.
- Topics sit in a 3 × 2 or 2-column grid. Each is either a 72 px icon over a 20 px title and 2–4 muted lines with no card, or a bordered card with an inset illustration panel.
- A label repeated on every card is noise.

**404 (1).** A mono eyebrow, a 2-line serif headline, one muted sentence and one pill button.

**Contact sales (1).** Underline-only fields with the question as the placeholder, and a full-width black pill.

**Calculator (1).**
- Uppercase 12 px labels.
- A segmented control and a slider, with the value right-aligned on the label row.
- A 96 px figure in a black card, and a breakdown set as key–value rows on hairlines.

**Bot checks (2).** These are system-font interstitials, not the product's design. Discard them.

## Profile: team cards and resource card rows (4)

**(counts)** Cards 75%. Inline actions in all 4.

**(notes)**
- Links are **pinned to one baseline at the card bottom** whatever the copy length (2).
- Team cards: a 96 px avatar, the name at 20 px, muted paragraphs, and a "More about X ›" link.
- Each card takes its own pastel tint while the layout stays the same (1).

## Dashboard: figure rows (8)

**(counts)**
- **Actions:** none 63%.
- **Spacing:** **160 px** between groups (the most of any kind), 20 px within, 36 px padding.
- **Headings:** 64% carry a description of 21 words. Titles have a median of 5 words.

**(notes)**
- **A 40–64 px figure over a 2–3-line muted caption at 14–18 px, with no label and no icon** (3).
- The figure is regular or light in 3, so size alone carries it. It is a serif in 2 and an accent or gradient fill in 3.
- The figures sit on equal cards of one height (3) or in columns split by vertical rules (2).

## Form (5)

**(counts)**
- **Fields:** labelled 67%, placeholder only 33%, with helper text 27%.
- **Actions:** inline 80%, right-aligned at the foot 20%.
- **Spacing:** 36 px between groups (the tightest of any kind), 16 px within, 24 px padding.
- **Headings:** titles have a median of 7 words, phrased as questions.

**(notes)**
- Fields are filled light grey, borderless or with a hairline, 48–55 px tall at a 12 px radius (3).
- Labels are questions at 15 px medium, with an 11–13 px muted helper about 8 px below (2).
- "(optional)" is set inline after the label in a lighter weight (1).
- Radios are drawn like the fields, as full-width grey pills (1).
- **The submit is not full width** in 3: a compact centred pill, a half-width right-aligned button, or a button in the composer's corner.

## Settings (1)

A misfiled security section: two navy cards with no border, each holding a visual, a two-colour 40 px title and a 20 px muted body. No rules can be drawn from it.

## Across the sections of a landing page

## Grouping
**(counts)** Mixed 40%, space 27%, cards 22%, dividers 10%.

**(notes)**
- Sections are told apart by a **band of a new colour** (≈30), by 120–300 px of space (≈12), or by a hairline. Where a hairline is used it keeps 30–80 px on each side (6). The spacing skill has the rule replace the gap.
- Cards hold **parallel items**: plans, bento tiles, testimonials, help topics. A section is not a card. When a section does sit on a surface, it is a large rounded panel inset 8–20 px from the viewport, stacked like sheets (≈10).
- Dividers belong to long lists of one kind: FAQ rows, comparison rows, article lists, the legal row.

## Descriptive text
**(counts)**
- 55% of headings carry a description, with a median of **17 words under a 3-word title**.
- Marketing runs 65%, navigation 21%.

**(notes)**
- Descriptions are muted and capped at about 450–700 px.
- **Two-tone text** turns a title and its description into one line or paragraph (≈20). That saves a block.
- **Eyebrows** appear in ≈40 marketing notes. Some state a fact (news, a step number, a rating). Others repeat the heading ("FAQ" over "FAQs"), and those are noise.
- Captions sit over logo strips, under CTAs and under figures. Each one has to add something the content does not show.

## Spacing
**(counts)**
- 16 px within a group, 80 px between groups, 28 px padding: about **1 : 5 : 1.75**.
- Within-group spacing holds at 16–20 px in every kind. Between-groups runs from 36 px (forms) to 160 px (figure rows).
- Padding runs from 24 px (marketing) to 48 px (footers).

**(notes)**
- Bento and feature cards are padded 32–64 px.
- **Cards sit closer to each other (16–24 px) than their own padding (40–64 px)**, so a set reads as one (2).
- Display headlines sit 10–40 px above their subtitles.

## Action hierarchy and placement
**(counts)** Inline 59%, none 30%, header 10%, full-width footer about 1%. A footer-right action never appears outside forms.

**(notes)**
- The primary is a **filled pill**, often near-black. On a dark ground it inverts to white (≈6).
- The secondary is **the same height and shape**, outlined or pale grey.
- The main action repeats in the nav, the hero and the close: one action, kept within reach.
- Smaller actions are **text links ending in an arrow** (≈20 standing in for a button), sometimes with the arrow inside a small circle (≈8). They name where they go.

## Type scale
**(counts)** A median of **5 sizes and 2 weights**. Marketing uses 3 weights.

**(notes)**

| Role | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Display | 48–110 px |
| Section heading | 36–56 px |
| Card or row title | 17–28 px |
| Body | 15–22 px |
| Small | 11–14 px |

- Display type has tight negative tracking and a line height near 1.0 (≈20).
- **A serif display face** appears in ≈40 notes, almost always over a sans body and interface. A serif in body copy, descriptions or links (≈7) goes against the typography skill and the principles.
- Mono carries eyebrows, indices, stat captions and legal lines in ≈15 notes, including on non-technical products.
- Small labels are uppercase and tracked.
- Warm off-white and cream grounds appear in ≈20 notes.

## Icons: where they go and where they do not
**(counts)** Decorative 54%, in buttons 50%, in navigation 31%, in list rows 30%, on labels 19%, on headers 16%, **leading a field 9%**.

**Where icons go (notes):**
- trailing arrows or chevrons on buttons and links
- checks in plan lists
- dropdown chevrons
- the expand glyph on FAQ rows
- the search magnifier

**Where icons do not go (notes):**
- footer link columns, apart from one social column
- FAQ questions
- nav text links
- table rows, apart from check, dash and info glyphs

These shares are common, not a licence. The iconography skill starts an expressive page with no icons, and the principles cut an icon beside every label.

## What the accent marks (notes)
- **The primary CTA**, though in ≈8 notes it is near-black instead.
- **One word or line of a headline** (≈12), never more than one.
- Eyebrows and section indices (≈10).
- Links, expand glyphs, and the selected tab or segment.
- **Whole bands** set in the brand colour as a surface (≈8).
- Per-item pastel tints that tell parallel cards apart under one layout (≈10).
- Tinted "NEW" pills (≈6). The color skill's neutral badge holds for status.

Logos stay mostly monochrome. Gradients fill panels behind mocks (≈10) and occasionally a headline word (≈4), never a button.
