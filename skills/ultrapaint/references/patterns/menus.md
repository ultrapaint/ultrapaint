# Menus: command palettes (~36 notes), mega menus (~13), account menus (~10), row and dropdown menus (~8)

These figures show how one reference library builds menus. They are counted over the 66 menu pieces in a study of 575. A share is a share of the menu pieces. "~n notes" means at least n, because the notes never record an absence. A figure describes what is common, not what is right. Where a figure differs from the design skills, the skills win, because they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared. Where either one differs from the person's principles, the principles win.

**All menus**
- 48% group with mixed means, 42% with dividers.
- 89% put icons on list rows.
- 42% of choices carry a description line.
- 17% of headers carry a description. Titles run 2 words.
- 3 type sizes, 2 weights: 12 px labels, 14 px rows, 16 px medium titles.
- Padding is 16. Space is 8 within a group and 24 between groups, a ratio of 1 : 3.
- 76% of fields are placeholder-only. These are the search fields.
- The menu opens 4 px off its trigger.

**Command palettes and search menus**
- Search:
  - a borderless band at the top: a leading magnifier, text larger than the rows, and a ⌘K or ⌘/ keycap at the right (~30 notes);
  - a full-width rule under it (~30).
- Rows are one line (~12):
  - a 20–24 px outline icon;
  - a label;
  - a right-aligned shortcut in bordered keycaps. A chord reads "⌘K → P".
- Group labels are small and muted, not bold (~20). They are sentence case in ~8 and uppercase in ~6. A second group often has no label, only its divider (~4).
- Hover and active rows get a rounded grey fill inset from the panel edges, never edge to edge (~22).
- The footer is a keyboard legend: a keycap, then a muted verb ("esc close", "↑↓ navigate", "↵ open") (~16).
- A long list fades at the bottom instead of being cut hard (~7).
- The matched part of each result gets a grey or pale accent highlight (3).
- Empty state (~8):
  - Centred. The illustration shows what the search would have found (an avatar fan, logos on rings), then a bold title.
  - A muted line of up to two lines quotes the query.
  - One secondary "Clear search" button.
  - In ~4 the description also repeats the recovery as an inline link. That is common, but it gives one action twice; keep the button alone.
- A count over results that are all visible ("Search results (4)") appears in 2. It restates the list.

**Mega menus**
- Each item (13 of 13):
  - a 20 px line icon or a small bordered tile;
  - a 16 px medium title;
  - a 14 px muted description of two lines, indented under the title, not under the icon.
- A tile before every item is common (~20 across menus and similar lists). By the principles it is decoration; a plain line icon or no icon does the job.
- Hover fills the whole item as a rounded block (~11).
- Column labels are 12 px uppercase, tracked and muted (~9).
- A side column on a grey fill holds a promo, a video or single-line links, and ends in a full-width outlined button (~10).
- An outlined "We're hiring!" pill sits inline after a title (~9).
- The open trigger's chevron flips up (~10).

**Account menus**
- The header row is an avatar with a status dot, then the name over a muted email.
- Accounts are radio rows with the radio at the top right. The current account is filled (~9).
- A label such as "Switch account" over the account rows appears in ~4. The rows already say what they are, so it can go.
- Action rows are an outline icon plus a label. "My profile" carries the @handle in muted text (~5).
- Sign out comes last, sometimes with a trailing icon. "Sign out of all" is a separate full-width outlined button (~4).
- In ~4 only Sign out has an icon. Icons are all or none within a group.

**Notification menus (2, with ~6 panels elsewhere)**
- A 40 px avatar sits in a fixed left gutter.
- The first line is a sentence: the actor and the object in semibold, the verb regular and muted.
- A muted line follows with the time and the team.
- Attachments and Accept/Decline buttons line up with the text column, not with the avatar.
- Unread is a small dot, not a row fill.

**Row and dropdown menus**
- A kebab at the row's end opens a menu of icon-plus-label items (~8), and the hovered item is filled.
- The destructive item comes last, below a divider, in red text (~5).
- In ~3 one group of items has icons and another group of links has none. Keep each group to one style.
