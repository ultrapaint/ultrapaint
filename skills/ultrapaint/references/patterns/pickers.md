# Pickers: calendars and date pickers

How one UI kit builds these screens, counted over the 14 pieces of this kind in a study of 575. About 10 of them are calendars and 3 are date pickers. A share is of this kind's pieces, and "~n notes" means at least n. Where the kit differs from the design skills, the skills win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**Calendar header**
- A date tile (the month abbreviated over the day number), then the title, then a muted date range.
- 64% of these pieces put actions in the header. From the left, they run: Prev / Today / Next joined as one group, then a view select, then "Add event" (~8 notes). The joined group appears in all 8.

**Events**
- The tint, border and text all take one hue per category (~8). These category hues are a separate set from the accent.
- The title sits on the left and is truncated; the time sits on the right.
- A cell shows at most 3 events, then a muted "N more…" (4).

**Time and days**
- The current time is a dotted red line with its label in the gutter (4).
- The selected day is solid black and today is a grey circle (~5). Neither one uses the accent.
- Days outside the month are faded or filled grey.

**Date pickers (2 notes)**
- Presets sit in a side column.
- The footer repeats the range in two inputs joined by "–", with Cancel and Apply on the right.

**Spacing**
- 6 within groups, 24 between, padding 24. That is a 1 : 4 ratio, wider than the 1 : 2.7 across all kinds.
