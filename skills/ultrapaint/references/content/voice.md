# Voice

How the product speaks and what it calls things: one voice on every screen, its tone moving with
the moment. What the product chose is in DESIGN.md; what follows holds where it chose nothing.

## The product's choices come first

Read the copy the product already has before writing: its screens and the strings in its code.
The voice DESIGN.md sets is the voice: the tone in its Overview (the character's Tone dial:
terse, warm or the brand's), and in its Content section what this product chose: how its words
sound, the one name of each thing and the words it never uses for it, its formats. Plain language
bends to it everywhere except errors and destructive moments. Where DESIGN.md chose nothing, the
defaults of the content references hold.

When you make the design system, write the product's choices in DESIGN.md's Content section,
taken from the copy it already has. A rule that holds for any product stays in these references.

## One voice, a tone for the moment

| Moment | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Success, onboarding, empty states | Warm, may be light |
| Routine actions, settings | Neutral, minimal |
| Errors, destructive confirmations | Calm and plain |
| Data loss, security, money | Serious and explicit |

## One name per thing

If it is a workspace in the menu, it is a workspace in the toast. A flow keeps one vocabulary:
"Continue" or "Next", and "Done" to finish.

## Speak to the reader

"You", not "the user". No gender by default, no idioms that need a culture to decode, "select"
when both touch and pointer apply.
