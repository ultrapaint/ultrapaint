# Page copy

A public page's words: its headline, subhead and eyebrow, its actions and its prices. Read
`references/studies/content.md` when writing them. It holds the headline, subhead, eyebrow and
action counts for each character, with recipes for heroes, pricing, shops, title screens,
statement headlines and editorial fronts.

## A page's first words

On a public page, design and test with a headline of about 5 words: about 6 for a technical
product, about 4 for an expressive site, where the name alone often stands. A subhead is one
sentence of about 15 words. Write one only when it adds what the headline cannot, such as who it
is for, the price or how it works. Otherwise the headline stands alone. There is no eyebrow by
default. Keep one only when it says what the heading does not: news, proof, a step number or a
card's category.

## A price where the decision is made

A price sits in the button or the sentence beside it ("Add to bag – €66"). A sale price sits
beside its struck-through original.

## Before you finish

| You see | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A subhead that restates the headline, an eyebrow naming what its heading names | Delete it |
| A price far from its button | In the button or the sentence beside it |
