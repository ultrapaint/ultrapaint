# Usability

Nielsen's heuristics for a flow of steps or a tool used daily: people can tell where they are and
what happened, leave or undo what they started, recognise rather than remember, and go faster the
tenth time. Run them over every screen of the flow and the states between. Status, the user's
words, minimalism, error recovery and labels are the core's States, Words, Restraint and
Accessibility rules; the five below add their detail.

## Every way in has a way out

A dialog closes with Escape, its close button and Cancel; a step has Back; a mode (editing,
selecting, full screen) shows how to leave it. Filters and a selection clear in one action. Cancel
leaves no trace.

## One thing, one name, one place

The primary sits on the same side of every footer; Save stays Save on the next form. The
platform's conventions hold over invention: the logo goes home, a link looks like a link, a
checkbox is a checkbox.

## Constraints before error messages

Where the valid set is known, offer it: a date picker, a select, a stepper for a count. Fill in
the default most people want. Accept a format as typed (a phone number, a card number with
spaces) and normalise it.

## Options in view

The plan's price sits by the button that buys it, the item's name in its confirmation. Recent and
suggested items lead a search or a picker. Every action has a visible control; a shortcut is the
second way to it.

## Faster the tenth time

A tool used daily shows its shortcuts in its menu items and tooltips, takes bulk actions on a
selection, and, with many commands, has a command menu (`⌘K`). A first visit sees none of it on
the way.

## Help at the point of doubt

A field or setting that needs explaining gets one line of helper text or a "Learn more" link
beside it, where the question comes; the rest of the screen stays bare. A help centre leads with
search.

## Check

| You see | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| An action reachable only by a shortcut | A visible control, the shortcut shown beside it |
| Filters or a selection cleared one by one | One action that clears them |
| Cancel that keeps part of what was entered | Cancel leaves no trace |
| The primary on the left of one footer and the right of the next | One side on every screen |
| A help icon on every label | Helper text where a rule needs it; nothing elsewhere |
