# Patterns

How a well-made product builds each kind of screen, counted: what its dialogs, settings pages,
menus, dashboards, tables, forms, navigation and marketing pages hold, how they group it, how
much text they carry and where their actions go. Read the one for the screen at hand before
placing a node; the skills still decide where the two differ.

## A pattern is a starting point

A share says what is usual, not what is right: the design skills hold what a person preferred
when the two were compared, and where they differ the skills win. The less-is-more rules apply to
what a pattern adds: a description under every heading is common, and still cut where the heading
already says it.
