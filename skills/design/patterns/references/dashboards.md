# Dashboards

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**KPI cards (~28 notes)**
- The card holds:
  - a small muted label, often with a dropdown caret;
  - a kebab menu top-right;
  - a large bold number;
  - a delta pill (green arrow and %) at the bottom-right or beside the number;
  - often a sparkline.
- The label sits **above** the number in every case noted.
- One card is marked selected or active with a fill, an inversion to black, or an underline (~6 notes).

**The page figure**
- When the page has one hero figure (balance, revenue), it is very large: about 40–100 px in ~8 notes. A smaller muted unit or currency sits beside it.

**Header**
- A breadcrumb above a large title, sometimes with a one-line muted description (only 15% have a description).
- 55% place actions in the header: right-aligned on the title line.
- In ~6 notes **all header buttons are outlined secondary, with no filled primary**.

**Time range**
- A segmented control "12 months / 30 days / 7 days / 24 hours" with a raised or filled active segment (~15 notes).
- Or plain text "12m 30d 7d 24h", where the active value is dark and bold and the rest grey (~7).
- It sits at the right of the section title, next to a Filters button.

**Sections**
- Grouping is 43% mixed, 22% cards, 23% dividers, 12% space.
- KPI cards are bordered, while the chart and the table often sit directly on the page (~12 notes).
- A section title over a full-width rule replaces a card in ~6 notes.
- Card-in-card appears in ~6 notes: a tinted title strip over an inner white panel.

**Charts (~12 notes)**
- Monochrome or one hue: a solid current line, a dotted or dashed previous period, a hatched forecast region.
- A dark tooltip.
- Faint gridlines and sparse axis labels, with no legend box.

**Tables in dashboards**
- Checkbox; avatar or logo with name over a muted email or domain; a status pill with a dot; a progress bar with %; a kebab or trash and pencil icons.
- Selecting rows turns the header into "3 selected" (the count in the accent colour) and raises a **floating dark bulk-action bar** (Export, Archive, Delete) (~7 notes).
- Section titles carry an inline muted count or delta: "Customers 68", "Site traffic +104%" (~5).

**Search empty states (~11 notes)**
- Centred: an illustration (rings, an avatar cluster or a photo stack), a bold title, a two-line muted explanation that **echoes the query** and ends in an underlined link, then one outlined "Clear search", sometimes beside a primary.

**Spacing, type and icons**
- Padding 24, between 40, within 16.
- **5 type sizes, 3 weights**, the most of any app kind.
- Leading icons in fields: 71% (search fields).
