# Marketing pages

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**Hero**
- Centred: an announcement pill with a link (~12 notes), a 2-line very large headline, a 1–2-line grey subhead, and a button pair, secondary (often with a play icon) then primary (~15).
- One italic serif word inside a sans headline, or a gradient or hand-drawn accent on one word (~14).

**Sections**
- Each section opens with a small accent-coloured eyebrow ("Features", "Pricing") above the heading (~10).
- 70% of headers have a description (about 8 words).
- Between groups 64, padding 32. Grouping is space only in 58%.

**Primary buttons**
- 2 primaries per piece is typical: the nav CTA repeats the hero CTA.

**Pricing (~14 notes)**
- Each card follows one order: name, one-line audience, a huge price with a small "per month", a full-width button, a divider, a "FEATURES" label, then a checklist.
- The recommended plan is shown by a fill (accent, dark or grey) or a "Popular" pill. A stronger border is rare.
- Discounts show a struck-through old price above the new one (3).
- The comparison table's columns line up under the cards. Its cells are a check, a dash or a short value, and each row label has a small help icon (~5).

**FAQ (~14 notes)**
- A narrow centred column, or a two-column split with the title left and the questions right.
- Each row is a question, with a plus/minus or chevron at the right. Rows are separated by hairlines or by space alone.
- An open answer is in muted grey.
- The list fades into a "Show more" pill.

**Blog**
- Borderless image-on-top cards: image, title with a small arrow, a 3-line clamped excerpt, outlined pill tags.
- Underlined inline links, drop caps, a sticky table of contents.

**Social proof and palette**
- A greyscale logo strip with faded edges under a muted caption (~12 notes).
- Many pieces are single-hue or monochrome: one ink colour for text, borders, buttons and duotone photos (~10 notes).
