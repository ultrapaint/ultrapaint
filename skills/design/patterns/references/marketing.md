# Marketing pages

How the studied library builds this kind of page, counted over the 67 marketing pieces in a study of 575. A share is a share of those 67. "~n notes" means at least n, because the notes never record an absence. Where this differs from the design skills, the skills win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared. A common pattern is not therefore right.

**Hero (~20 notes)**
- The headline is 2 lines at about 48–72 px with tight tracking (~15). Under it sits an 18–20 px subhead, capped at about 520–760 px wide (~8).
- The two buttons are the same height. An outlined secondary with a leading play icon ("Demo", "How it works") sits on the left and the filled primary on the right (~14). On mobile they stack full width, primary first (3).
- The product screenshot is the hero image, often bleeding off an edge (~8).
- A serif or italic-serif word or headline sits over sans body text (~15). Each headline has one accent: a gradient word, a scribble underline or an italic word.

**Sections**
- 63% of headers have a description (median 10 words) under a title of about 3 words.
- An eyebrow, announcement pill or tag sits above the headline in ~25 notes. It is common by habit. The skills leave it out unless the brief asks for one.
- Padding is 32, with 16 within groups and 48 between them (about 1 : 3). Grouping is space alone in 39% and mixed in 40%.
- The median piece uses 5 type sizes and 3 weights, against 4 and 2 on a product screen.
- 61% have decorative icons. Hand-drawn arrows and scribbles (~8) and faint gridded hero backgrounds (~8) are common too. A landing page may take this freedom, but each piece of decoration still has to earn its place.

**Primary buttons**
- The median is 2 filled primaries per piece, because the nav CTA repeats the hero CTA.
- Actions sit inline in 58% and in the header in 27%.

**Pricing (~13 notes)**
- The price is very large, with a small muted "per month" on its baseline (~11).
- A full-width button sits at the bottom of each card, so the buttons line up (~10).
- A Monthly/Annual segmented control switches the prices (~7).
- Discounts show a struck-through old price above the new one (3). The current plan keeps a muted "Current plan" button (3).
- Tiers are cumulative. A "FEATURES" overline and "Everything in X plus…" open the list (3), and inherited checks are greyed (2). The overline names a list the checks already show.
- The popular plan is marked one way (a tint strip, a filled card or a "Popular" badge) in ~5 notes. In ~4 it is marked two or three ways at once. One way is enough.
- Comparison tables (~5): the cards head the value columns, and the cells hold a check-circle, an em dash or short text. Each feature label carries a help icon, which principle 1 counts as noise.

**FAQ (~10 notes)**
- Each row is a question in medium or semibold, with a plus/minus or chevron at the right. The open answer is muted grey.
- Rows are split by hairlines (~4) or by space alone (~5). In 2 the open item is a filled rounded block.
- The list fades into a "Show more" or "Load more" pill (~4).

**Blog (~22 notes)**
- Posts have no card surface: image, meta, a title with ↗, an excerpt clamped to 2–3 lines, and outlined pill tags (~8). Rules or space take the place of cards.
- In ~4 the meta is laid over a blurred strip on the image.
- A drop cap opens the article (~5). Links are underlined in the text's own ink (~6).

**Social proof and palette**
- A greyscale logo strip follows the hero (~8).
- Some blog pages are a single ink on cream or white (~5).
- Halftone logo watermarks appear in ~4.
