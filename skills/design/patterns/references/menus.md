# Menus: command palettes (~32 notes), mega menus (~15), account menus (~11), row and dropdown menus (4)

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**All menus**
- 50% group with dividers, 29% space, 17% mixed, 5% cards.
- 89% put icons on list rows.
- Choices with a description: 44%, almost all in mega menus.
- Headers rarely carry a description (13%).
- 3 type sizes, 2 weights. Padding 24, between 24, within 12.

**Command palettes**
- Search input:
  - at the top, **borderless**, with a leading magnifier and a ⌘K or ⌘/ keycap at the right (~20 notes);
  - a hairline below it (~20);
  - placeholder-only (75% of menus are placeholder-only).
- Groups are titled by small muted grey labels, not bold (~18 notes; uppercase in ~5). A text action ("Clear", "Customize") sits at the right of a group label.
- Rows:
  - a leading outline icon, avatar or bordered icon tile;
  - a label, sometimes with the handle or domain in lighter grey on the same line;
  - trailing items right-aligned: a keycap shortcut, a count badge, a type tag, or an avatar stack.
- Hover and highlight is a light grey rounded fill inset from the panel edge, with no border (~28 of 32).
- A footer bar of keyboard hints shows a keycap followed by a muted verb: navigate, open, esc close (~15 notes).
- The page behind is dimmed and blurred (~12 notes).
- The typed query is highlighted in the results with a pale tint (~5 notes).
- The empty state is centred: illustration or avatar cluster, a bold title, a muted line that quotes the query with an underlined link, and one outlined "Clear search" button (~5 notes).

**Mega menus**
- Each item is an icon (often in a small bordered square tile), a medium or bold title, and a **two-line grey description** (~12 of 15). Integration menus use a logo + name + muted domain instead (3).
- Columns are headed by small uppercase tracked grey labels, with only space between columns (~9).
- Hover is a light grey rounded fill.
- The open trigger gets a pill fill and its chevron flips up (~10). The page behind is dimmed (~8).
- A tinted or grey promo or quick-links panel sits at the right (~10). An outlined "We're hiring!" badge sits inline after a title (~10).

**Account menus**
- Action rows are icon + label.
- A small muted or uppercase label introduces the accounts ("Switch account").
- Account rows are avatar, name over email, and a radio at the right; the selected row is filled grey (~10 of 11).
- A divider sits between accounts and actions.
- The menu closes with a full-width outlined "Sign out of all accounts" button (~4).
- In 3 notes only the last item (Sign out) has an icon.
- The menu is anchored to its trigger, aligned to the trigger's right edge.

**Row menus**
- Icon + label items, with the hovered item filled.
- The destructive item comes last, after a divider, in red or with a warning icon (~4).
