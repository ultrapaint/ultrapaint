# Profiles

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

- A cover banner with a circular or rounded-square avatar overlapping its bottom edge, plus a verified badge (~10 notes).
- A stats row of three cells, each a **small muted label above a bold number**, separated by thin vertical rules (~7).
- Actions are equal outlined buttons with leading icons (Follow, Message, Save to list) (~4).
- Tags are outlined chips that end in a "+" chip (~4).
- Experience rows: logo tile, role, then muted company and dates, with a pencil at the far right.
- Dotted dividers separate the sections inside the card (~6).
- Team sections are photo grids with no surface: photo, name, muted role (~9).
- Fields here are always labelled (91%), with helper text in 36%.
- 5 type sizes, 3 weights.
