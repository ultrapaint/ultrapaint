# Landing pages

How well-made SaaS landing pages are built, counted over 291 slices of 844 real sites (heroes,
feature sections, pricing, FAQs, testimonials, closing calls to action, footers). "(counts)" are
shares of the pieces of a kind or medians; "≈n notes" are at least about n. Where it differs from
the design skills, the skills win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

## Marketing sections (146)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** space 43%, cards 26%, mixed 23%, dividers 8%.
- **Actions:** inline 66%, none 20%, in a header 10%, in a footer 4%.
- **Headings:** 64% carry a description. Titles have a median of 4 words, descriptions 19 words.
- **Type:** a median of **5 sizes and 3 weights**.
- **Primary buttons:** a median of **2**. The notes show why: the same CTA appears in both the nav and the hero.
- **Spacing:** 80 px between groups, 26 px container padding, 16 px within a group, so between-groups is 5× within.
- **Icons:** decorative 62%, buttons 46%, navigation 34%, list rows 31%, labels 22%, headers 16%, inside fields 8%.
- **Fields:** placeholder only 65%, labelled 30%, with helper text 9%.
- **Nesting:** a median of 2 levels.

### Hero (≈40 notes)

**Order.** The order runs:
1. An optional announcement bar or pill.
2. The headline.
3. A grey subhead of 1–2 lines.
4. The CTAs.
5. An optional reassurance line.
6. The product visual or a logo strip.

**Alignment.** About 6 in 10 heroes are **centred** (≈24). The rest are left-aligned or a text/visual split (≈14).

**Headline.**
- Sizes noted are 48, 56, 60, 64, 72 and 80 px.
- It runs 2–3 lines, with tight tracking and manual line breaks.
- About 10 notes have a light-weight headline and about 15 a heavy one.

**Two-tone headline (≈25 notes across all kinds).** The second line or key phrase is set apart in one of two ways:
- muted grey ("Every. Time."), or
- the accent colour, or a gradient.

**CTAs.**
- A **pair** in ≈27 notes, mostly filled + secondary. The secondary is:
  - outlined (≈11)
  - a text link with an arrow (≈7)
  - a pale grey or tinted fill (≈6)
  - translucent on a photo (≈3)
- **Pill buttons** appear in ≈40 notes.
- A trailing arrow on the CTA is very common.

**Email capture.** An email field and its button fused into one pill or row appears in ≈10 notes, always placeholder only.

**Above the headline.**
- An announcement bar above the nav in ≈13 notes.
- A pill badge above the headline (rating, "Now live", news) in ≈10.

**Below the CTA.** Reassurance microcopy ("No credit card required", "2GB for free") in ≈7 notes.

**Product visual.** Cropped, bleeding off an edge, or faded out at the bottom in ≈25 notes. It is often on a tinted or gradient rounded panel, or inside a device or browser frame.

**Logo strip.** Always greyscale or tone-on-tone. It is either one row or a marquee faded at the edges (≈25 notes). It sits under a small "Trusted by" caption in ≈6.

**Nav bar.**
- Logo left, links in the centre or at the right.
- A ghost, outlined or plain-text "Log in" beside a filled CTA (≈12).
- A floating pill bar (≈6).
- The current page as a filled pill (3).

### Feature sections (≈50 notes)

**Zig-zag rows (≈12).**
- Text and visual swap sides on each row.
- The text column is vertically centred against the visual.
- The visual sits on a pastel or tinted rounded panel (≈15 notes), with a white mini-UI card on top.
- Rows are separated by whitespace only, about 100–160 px.

**Bento grids (≈10).**
- The layout is one wide card over two half-width cards, or 3 cards then 2.
- Gutters are 16–32 px and radii 12–40 px.
- Cards are mostly **filled a few % darker than the page, with no border**.
- Card text is a title plus 1–3 grey lines.
- The mockup is cropped at the card edge.

**Feature columns (≈10, 3–4 across).**
- Each column stacks a small icon or illustration, a bold title and 2–4 grey lines.
- There is no card in about 8 of the 10.

**Section header.** Two forms:
- **centred:** eyebrow, headline, grey subline
- **split (≈8):** a large headline on the left and a short grey paragraph on the right, baseline-aligned

**Selector beside a visual (≈8).** Tabs, an accordion or a numbered list drives the picture:
- The active item is at full strength.
- The rest fade to grey.

**Mockups.** Abstracted mini-UIs, faded at the edges, are shown instead of full screenshots (≈10).

### Pricing (≈12 notes)

**Card stack.** Every card in a set has the **same internal order** (all 12):
1. The name.
2. A large price with a small unit.
3. A one-line note.
4. The button.
5. A checklist.

**Buttons.**
- The button is full width in ≈6, pinned to the card bottom in ≈4.
- The featured plan gets the filled button and the others outlined buttons (≈3).

**Featured plan.** It is marked by:
- a 2 px accent outline or tint (≈4)
- a tag on or above the top edge (≈5)
- **not by size** (one exception: a raised, larger centre card)

**Billing toggle.** A monthly/yearly pill toggle sits above the cards in ≈5, often with a savings tag.

**Checklist and comparison.**
- Feature lists use check icons (≈8).
- A comparison table follows the cards in ≈4.

### FAQ (≈15 across kinds)

**Row style.**
- Hairline-divided rows in ≈10.
- A separate rounded filled or bordered card per question in ≈5.

**Toggle icon.**
- Trailing at the right in ≈14: a plus (≈9), a chevron (≈5), a circled arrow (≈1).
- One note has a leading plus.

**Size and layout.**
- Rows are 58–105 px tall, median about 80.
- Questions only; the answers stay hidden.
- Two layouts:
  - **two-column (≈5):** the title (and sometimes a CTA) on the left, the list on the right
  - **narrow centred column, 550–1030 px wide (≈5)**

### Testimonials (≈15 notes)

- The quote is the large text.
- The attribution is small and grey: the name, then the role and company.
- Layouts: masonry, a carousel with the next card peeking, or a staggered or tilted arrangement.
- Key phrases are highlighted with a marker in 2 notes.

### CTA bands (≈12 notes)

A full-width dark or saturated rounded band holds one of two arrangements:
- a headline on the left and **one** button on the right, or
- a centred headline over one button

## Navigation: footers and closing CTAs (43)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** space 58%, dividers 19%, mixed 19%, cards 5%.
- **Container padding:** 48 px.
- **Actions:** inline 60%, header 12%, none 28%.
- **Headings:** 24% carry a description. Titles have a median of 2 words.
- **Icons:** decorative 56% (wordmarks, illustrations), buttons 28%, list rows 21%, **navigation 9%**.
- **Fields:** placeholder only 86%, **labelled 0%**, with helper text 14% (newsletter fields).

**(notes)**

**Layout.**
- A brand column on the left (logo, sometimes a tagline or badges).
- **3–6 link columns** on the right, typically 4–5, often starting at the page midpoint.

**Column headings.** Small, uppercase or mono in ≈6. The pieces are split on contrast:
- a **muted heading over darker links** in ≈9
- a **dark or bold heading over muted links** in ≈11

**Links.**
- Regular weight, 13–18 px, with no icons.
- Rows are 24–56 px apart, most often 28–44 px.

**Legal row.** Copyright on the left; legal links, social glyphs or a language selector on the right.
- A **hairline above it in ≈11 of ≈30 footers**.
- Space only in about 15.

**Social icons.** Plain monochrome glyphs with no chrome (≈6).

**Extras.**
- A giant wordmark as the closer (≈4).
- A status pill with a dot (≈3).
- "NEW" pills beside links (≈5).

**Closing CTA above the footer (≈15).**
- A large headline with one button, or a filled + outlined pair.
- A newsletter capture as an inline field + button, placeholder only (≈4).

## Table: grids and comparison tables (37)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** dividers 41%, space 30%, cards 19%, mixed 11%.
- **Actions:** none 51%, inline 35%, header 11%.
- **Headings:** 44% carry a description. Titles 3 words, descriptions 15 words.
- **Spacing:** 60 px between groups, 24 px padding, 12 px within, so 5:1.
- **Icons:** decorative 38%, list rows 30%, labels 24%, buttons 14%.

**Comparison tables (≈10).**
- Hairlines between rows in all of them.
- Rows are 45–97 px tall, most often 50–70.
- Labels are left-aligned and values centred.
- Values are:
  - "included": a check
  - "not included": a dash or a blank, never the word
  - a limit: short text ("Unlimited")
- Labels carry tooltip cues in ≈6: an info icon or a dotted underline.
- The recommended or brand column is highlighted in ≈5 by a full-height band or an outline running through every row.
- Group headers are grey bands or larger titles.

**Feature grids (≈9).**
- Most are cells divided by hairlines inside one outer border (≈6). The rest use space only.
- Each cell runs icon or illustration, title, 2–4 grey lines.
- A "Learn more →" link is pinned to the bottom so links align across the row (≈3).

**Blog grids and lists (≈10).**
- 3 columns, with 16:9 to 16:10 thumbnails.
- A category chip and a grey date in a meta row.
- A medium title at 20–24 px, then a 2-line grey excerpt.
- No card surface in ≈6.
- List rows put the thumbnail beside the text, divided by hairlines.
- Three text levels by colour and size: date, title, excerpt.

**People, award and logo grids (≈5).**
- Centred stacks, or a three-step hierarchy: a small grey year, a large title, a small source.

## Other (43)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** space 49%, cards 26%, mixed 14%, dividers 12%.
- **Actions:** inline 51%, none 35%.
- **Headings:** 61% carry a description of 14 words.
- **Spacing:** 32 px padding, 80 px between groups, 14 px within.
- **Type:** 4 sizes.

**(notes)**

**Help centres (3).**
- A serif or large headline over a **pill search field with a leading magnifier**, placeholder only.
- Topic tiles: an icon or illustration, a title, 2–4 grey lines.

**Logo walls (≈5).**
- An equal-cell grid divided by 1 px borders or plus marks, or white tiles on a tinted page.
- Logos are monochrome.

**Section intros (≈4).**
- A tight medium-weight title of about 64 px with a 2-line grey paragraph, or a split title and paragraph.

## Profile: case-study, team and resource cards (10)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** cards 70%.
- **Actions:** inline 90%.
- **Icons:** in buttons 80% (arrows).
- **Headings:** 69% carry a description.
- **Spacing:** 40 px between groups, 24 px padding, 12 px within.

**(notes)**
- A 2–3 column grid of cards.
- Each card stacks an image or avatar, a small grey label, a title, 1–2 grey lines and a **text link with an arrow**. It is not a button in ≈7 of 10.
- The link is **pinned to the card bottom** in 4 of 10.
- Team grids use **greyscale portraits with identical framing** (2).

## Dashboard: stats rows (7)

**(counts)**
- **Grouping:** cards 43%, mixed 43%, dividers 14%.
- **Actions:** none 57%.
- **Type:** 5 sizes.
- **Spacing:** **120 px** between groups, 32 px padding.
- **Icons on headers:** 0%.

**(notes)**
- 3–4 stats in a row.
- A **big number over a small grey caption**. The number is light-weight at about 56 px, a serif, or heavy.
- Split by vertical rules (3), or set on cards (3).
- One weight only in 2: size and colour carry the hierarchy.
- In one note the caption repeats the number.

## Form (4)

**(counts)**
- **Fields:** labelled 79%, with helper text 36%, placeholder only 29%.
- **Actions:** full-width at the foot 25%, right-aligned at the foot 25%, inline 50%.
- **Spacing:** 10 px within a group, 38 px between groups, 40 px padding.
- **Text:** titles 6 words.
- **Type:** 5 sizes, 3 weights.

**(notes)**
- One column about 670 px wide, or a form in the right column beside an intro.
- A bold 16 px label above each field, with an "optional" hint on the same line (1).
- Fields about 55 px apart.
- Filled grey inputs (2).
- Choices as pill radios or a segmented toggle (2).
- First and last name share a row (1).

## Settings (1)

A misfiled security section: two dark cards, each with a graphic, a two-tone heading and grey body text. No rules can be drawn from it.

## Across the sections of a landing page

## Grouping
**(counts)**
- Space 43%, cards 24%, mixed 19%, dividers 14%.
- Dividers lead only where rows repeat: tables 41%, plus FAQs and footers.
- Cards lead in profile cards (70%) and stats.

**(notes)**
- The commonest card is a **tinted fill with no border** (≈20), then a hairline border (≈15), then a shadow (≈12, soft or a hard offset as a brand signature).
- Sections are told apart by **full-bleed colour-band changes** in ≈20 notes, in place of rules.

## Descriptive text
**(counts)**
- 54% of headings carry a description. It has a median of **19 words, against 4 for the title**, about 5:1.
- By kind: profile 69%, marketing 64%, other 61%, dashboard 54%, table 44%, form 33%, navigation 24%.
- Only 13% of fields have helper text.

**(notes)**
- The description sits directly under the heading, grey, 1–2 lines, or in the right column of a split header.
- Card bodies run 2–4 grey lines.
- **Eyebrows** above headlines appear in ≈55 notes, about 1 in 5. They are:
  - small uppercase or mono labels
  - tinted pills
  - numbered labels ("04 GOVERNANCE")

## Spacing
**(counts)**
- Within a group 16 px, between groups 70 px, container padding 28 px.
- So between-groups is **≈4.4× within**, and padding is ≈1.75× within.
- Marketing runs 5×. Stats sections go to 120 px between groups. Tables (12 px) and forms (10 px) are tighter within.

**(notes)**
- Sections are 100–160 px apart.
- Card padding is 40–80 px.
- Gutters are 16, 24 or 32 px.
- Stacked accordion cards sit 8–14 px apart.

## Action hierarchy and placement
**(counts)**
- Inline 58%, none 27%, header 10%, footer 4%.
- A median of 1 primary button per piece, **2 in marketing**.

**(notes)**
- The primary repeats in the nav, the hero and the closing band.
- Secondary styles: outlined, a text link with an arrow, a pale fill.
- Cards link with **text + arrow**, not buttons.
- Arrows trail the CTAs.

## Type scale
**(counts)**
- A median of **5 sizes and 2 weights**; marketing uses 3 weights.

**(notes)** Roughly five steps:
| Role | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Display | 48–80 px |
| Section heading | 32–56 px |
| Card title | 18–24 px |
| Body | 15–20 px (marketing body 17–20) |
| Small | 12–14 px |

- Large type is tight-tracked.
- Eyebrows and nav labels are uppercase or mono in ≈15 notes.
- Reading columns are 530–700 px wide (≈8).
- A serif display face appears in ≈60 notes, about 1 in 5.

## Icons: where they go and where they do not
**(counts)** Decorative 53%, buttons 40%, list rows 27%, navigation 22%, labels 18%, headers 13%, **inside fields 7%**.

**Where icons go (notes):**
- illustrations and 3D objects
- trailing arrows on buttons and links
- checks in pricing and benefit lists
- plus or chevron toggles on FAQ rows
- small icons over feature-column titles
- the magnifier in a search field

**Where icons do not go (notes):**
- footer links
- fields other than search
- stats
- most headings

## What the accent marks (notes)
- The **primary CTA**. In ≈6 notes the accent is explicitly the only saturated element.
- **One emphasis word or line in a headline** (≈20).
- Eyebrow text (≈10).
- Check icons in pricing lists (≈6).
- The recommended plan's outline or tint.
- "NEW" pills.
- Text links in cards.

Logos, chrome and body text stay neutral.
