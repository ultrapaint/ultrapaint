# Other: toasts, notification panels, AI chat, file managers, auth and contact pages, icon sets

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

This kind is a mix, so only the sub-patterns that repeat are reported.

**Toasts (~9 notes)**
- Leading icon, file tile or avatar; then a bold title and a one-line muted description; a close X at the top right.
- The actions are two text-only links, side by side and left-aligned under the text. The dismissive one is muted grey; the confirming one is bright or accent ("View in finder", "Undo"). They differ by tone only, with no button chrome.
- Often a dark inverted surface over a light page, or one step lighter than a dark page.

**Notification panels and popovers (~8 notes, plus 2 in menus)**
- Each row has an avatar column and a text column.
- The text is a sentence with the actor and the object in bold and the verb in regular weight, then a muted meta line ("2 min ago • Design").
- Attachments and quoted comments sit in small bordered or tinted inset cards. These are indented to the **text column, not the avatar** (stated in 6 notes).
- Unread is a small dot. A request shows inline Decline (outline) and Accept (filled) buttons.
- The tabs are a segmented control with a count badge on the active tab.

**AI chat panels (~8 notes)**
- Assistant messages sit left: grey-filled or white bordered bubbles. User messages are offset right in the other treatment.
- A dark floating icon toolbar appears over a hovered message.
- The composer has a tinted footer strip holding the model select, Shortcuts and Attach.
- Yes/no confirmations are two equal outlined buttons with a red ✕ and a green ✓ (4 notes), or two text-only actions.

**File managers (~5 notes)**
- A row of 4 equal quick-create cards: icon tile top-left, a plus top-right, the label at the bottom.
- Below them, a table whose rows are icon tile, then name over "size | type".
