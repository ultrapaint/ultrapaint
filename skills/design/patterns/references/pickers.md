# Pickers: calendars and date pickers

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**Calendar header**
- A month-over-day date badge, then the month title, then a muted date range.
- At the right, in order: search, a joined prev / Today / next control, a view select, and a dark "Add event" (~8 notes).

**Events**
- Chips tinted in their category's hue, with a darker text of the same hue and a matching border.
- In the week view, height equals duration. In the month view, the title is left and the time right, truncated with an ellipsis, with "N more…" as overflow (~10).

**Time and days**
- The current time is a dotted red line with a label in the gutter (~5).
- Today is a filled circle; out-of-month days are muted.

**Date pickers (~5 notes)**
- The selected day is a dark circle, hover a pale circle, and a dot marks a day with events.
- A range is drawn as filled endpoints joined by a pale band.
- Presets are listed on the left.
- The footer holds start and end inputs, then Cancel and Apply on the right.

**Spacing**
- Within 8, between 32, padding 24.
