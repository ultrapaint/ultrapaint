# Settings pages

How Untitled UI builds it, counted over the pieces of its kind in a study of 600 (a share is of
that kind's pieces; "~n notes" is at least n). Where it differs from the design skills, the skills
win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared.

**Layout**
- The two-column row is the dominant layout (~30 notes): label and one-line hint on the left (~310–400 px), control on the right (inputs ~600 px wide, leaving empty space at the right).
- The rows stack into one column on mobile (shown in ~8 notes).
- Variant: a three-column read-only row (~8 notes): label + hint | current value as plain text | an "Edit / Upload / Manage" text link or small outlined button. Values become inputs only when edited.

**Grouping**
- 53% dividers, 28% mixed, 19% cards, 0% space only.
- Rows are separated by full-width hairlines or dotted rules (dotted in ~12 notes).
- Where cards are used, it is one bordered card per topic: plan, payment method, usage, billing history (billing pages, ~8 notes). The card has a header strip (title + status pill + kebab) above an inset white body or a footer strip.

**Headers**
- Title of about 2 words; 61% have an ~8-word muted description.
- Section titles repeat the page header's pattern at a smaller size.
- A featured icon tile sits beside the title in ~5 notes.

**Actions**
- 44% inline, 28% header, 9% footer-right.
- Save and Cancel go right-aligned at the foot of a card or form after a hairline (~5 notes), or top-right in the section header (2).
- An autosave "Changes saved ✓" indicator replaces the Save button in 3 notes.

**Fields**
- 36% labelled, 57% placeholder-only, 6% helper text.
- In the two-column layout the row label is the field's label, so inputs carry no label of their own.
- URL fields take a fused grey prefix addon ("untitledui.com/") (~5 notes). Textareas show a character counter under them (~3).

**Required and verification**
- Required fields get a small asterisk in the accent colour (blue, indigo or green) after the label (~6 notes).
- Email verification opens inline as a tinted or bordered card inside the row: 4–6 code boxes, a link and a Verify button (~9 notes).

**Tabs**
- The tabs under the page title are one of three styles: a segmented pill track with a raised white chip (~12 notes), an underline (~8), or a filled pill (~8).
- The app shell is usually an icon rail plus a labelled settings sub-nav whose active item is a filled row (~12 notes).

**Billing (~19 notes)**
- The price is a large figure with a small muted unit on its baseline: "$40 per month", "60.2GB of 200GB" (~12 notes).
- The current plan's button is outlined, greyed or disabled ("Current plan"), while the others are filled or read "Upgrade" (~8 notes).
- A selected plan or payment card gets a thick dark or accent border and a filled check (~7 notes).
- History headings carry a muted count ("Billing history 44").
- Selecting rows turns the table header into "2 invoices selected".

**Integrations (~8 notes)**
- A 3-column grid of bordered cards, each with:
  - logo + name top-left and a toggle top-right;
  - a 1–2-line description;
  - a footer strip split off by a hairline, with a text link or a "Connections" button and a gear.
- The toggle is on in the accent colour or near-black, and off in pale grey.

**Spacing and type**
- Padding 24, between groups 40, within a group 16. That is 2.5× between groups versus within.
- 4 sizes, 2 weights.
