# Settings pages

How one widely used UI kit builds it, counted over the 65 settings pieces in a study of 575. A share is of those pieces. "~n notes" means at least n, because the notes never record an absence. Where the kit differs from the design skills, the skills win: they hold what a person preferred when the two were compared. The principles win over both, so a pattern here is common, not right.

**Layout**
- The two-column row is the dominant layout (~26 notes). Label and muted hint sit on the left, taking about a third (~270–300 px). The control sits on the right, capped at about 512 px.
- On mobile the rows stack into one column (~7 notes).
- Variant: a read-only row (~9 notes, plus 3 profile pieces). It reads label | stored value as plain text | an underlined "Edit" link or a small outlined Edit button flush right.
- Rows hold values the user edits. Records the user picks among (plans, payment methods, integrations) are cards: billing (~20 notes) and integrations (~8).

**Grouping**
- 51% mixed, 29% dividers, 20% cards, 0% space only. This is the only kind in the study never held by space alone.
- A hairline separates the rows (~26 notes). Dotted rules replace solid ones in ~13.
- The section header sits outside its card, or in the left column (~8 notes).
- On billing pages, a card's header strip holds the title, an inline status badge and a kebab (~5 notes). Across the study, ~12 pieces draw a card twice: a grey frame around an inset white panel. That is common here; the skills draw each edge once.

**Headers**
- Titles run about 2 words. 69% carry a muted description of about 9 words, and sections carry 2–3 lines of hint beside them. That makes the description a default rather than something that earned its place. Cut any line that restates its heading.
- A featured icon tile sits in the gutter left of a section title in ~5 notes, and the rows line up with the title text, not the icon. By the principles the tile is decoration.
- Row and choice title lines are semibold (~5 notes). Field labels are medium weight (~6, across the study).

**Actions**
- Save and Cancel go right-aligned in a footer on each card after a divider (~3 notes), or in the section header (1).
- In ~4 notes an autosave "Changes saved ✓" replaces the Save button.
- In ~30 notes across the study the primary button is near-black, and the accent is kept for selection.

**Fields**
- 62% labelled; helper text 5%. Where helper text appears, it carries live data or rules, not instructions.
- A toggle sits at the row's right in ~8 notes. It sits before its label in ~6, and every one of those rows either ends in another control or is an option card.
- Three "default apps" pieces repeat "Open with" and "View in" beside every row's select, under a column that already names them. The principles count that as noise.

**Verification**
- Email verification opens in place under the email row as a bordered card (~7 notes). It holds an icon, a title, a hint, 4–6 code cells, Verify and a resend link.

**Tabs**
- The sub-navigation under the title takes one of four styles:
  - a segmented track with a white raised chip (~14 notes);
  - an underline (~8);
  - plain text with a grey pill on the active tab (~5);
  - outlined pills with a black active tab (3).
- 80% of settings pieces carry icons in their navigation.

**Billing (~20 notes)**
- The price is a large figure with a small muted unit on its baseline: "$40 per month", "60.2GB of 200GB" (~13 notes).
- Plan cards stack name, spec and price, then a full-width button. The current plan reads "Current plan", outlined or disabled, and only the plan you can move to gets the filled button (~7).
- Across the study, a chosen card takes a 2 px accent border and a filled check (~30 notes), and an unchosen one keeps an empty circle in the same spot. Marking it with tint, coloured text, border and check at once (~4) says one thing four times.
- The history title carries its count in muted text: "Billing history 44" (~6).
- Bulk selection turns the table header into "N invoices selected" (~4).
- Pastel tier tints appear on billing cards (~3). That is warmer than a technical tool asks for.
- One billing table sets every cell in one muted size and weight. It is the only break from data hierarchy that the notes record.

**Integrations (~8 notes)**
- A grid of bordered cards, each with these parts (8/8):
  - a logo tile and the name, with a toggle top-right;
  - a 1–2-line description, clamped so the cards match in height;
  - a footer under a divider, with a text link or "Connections" plus a gear.

**Spacing and type**
- Padding 24, between groups 32, within a group 12. That is about 2.7× between groups versus within.
- 4 sizes, 2 weights. Across the study, rows and descriptions are 14 px and uppercase labels are 12 px.
